## The recognition
The whole app is one Meru — the Śrī Yantra raised into its three-dimensional mountain. Seen from above it is the Mandala; tilted, the same form is the Climb. Going inward through the nine āvaraṇas is going upward toward the Bindu.

- **The Gates** (base, life size): today's Śakti meets you; the Rite. S14 Rite inside S1 Door.
- **The terraces** (āvaraṇas 1–9): each a world (Ring Worlds restored); Śaktis seated as lights; the 33 chakras where canon B places them. S12 Axis.
- **Rooms**: a seat → her Home; a chakra light → its own room. S13 Room, made by rule (33), four by hand.
- **Membranes**: Shadow 3→4, Armin 6→7 — S11 Crossing, G15 membrane push; Shadow darkens the whole Meru until Armin.
- **The summit**: the Bindu — G2 stillness gate, G17 the two holds; Mauna; her source; Panel 9 → "Now go play".
- **Lalitā's field**: through the summit; each game its own world (play chassis). S17 → S8.
- **The sky**: the record as stars, day glyph, month letter, year ring, five clocks. S16 Portrait.
- **Under the ground**: the Well, letters (A3), witness, Shadow Translator. S15 Mirror, G14 descent.

Navigation: no hamburger, no ✶ ring — vertical drag climbs; tilt turns Mandala ↔ Climb; fly in through the point; leave by the left edge; modes by the Codex gestures; the rope; the Veil as the one index; the die.

2. Data rendered as prose — one ScrollView template for all 33 chakra rooms.
3. The loom skipped — no brief, recognition, five-track score or room test per world.
4. Tests checked laws, not experience — no parity pass against the Claude Design exports.
5. Every new world drawn flat in Canvas, though Metal/SceneKit engines exist.

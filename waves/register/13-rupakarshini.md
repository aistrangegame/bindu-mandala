**What this material is, in one paragraph.** rupakarshini is the form-giving engine: `OUTPUT = CHASSIS ⊗ WORLD ⊗ CONTAINER ⊗ CONTENT`
(SK:25). A *chassis* is a whole visual language (type pairing, grounds, accent, pace, sound root). Thirteen "studio" chassis were
distilled on 30 Sept 2026 from the eleven Claude Design exports (140 prototypes), and six of them were made from exactly this app's
material or its siblings: **mandala** (Bindu Mandala itself), **chakra** (Chakra Game), **lalita** (Lalita Family), **leela**
(Leela on the 33), **play** (the playing side), **ascent** (Bindu Practice). The other seven (feed, candle, learning, being, blink,
field, linen) come from Bindu Feed, A Strange I Game, the ASG Learning App, Bindu Being, Bindu Blink and the Bindu Field App.
`EC` holds ~260 rebuild-grade elements (every number needed to port them). `SH` holds seventeen shapes, six of which (S12 Axis,
S13 Room, S14 Rite, S15 Mirror, S16 Portrait, S17 Instrument Room) were written from this app's Homes, Living Rite, Mirror, Memory
Layer and Instrument Room. The thirteen studio proof builds are token proofs (the same eight-band page worn in each language), not
shape builds. **Nothing in the corpus has yet been ported to SwiftUI**; Part B is that port.

## PART C · CONFLICTS AND GAPS

1. **One 8 s breath clock vs laws 7–8.** `Theme/BreathClock.swift` snaps every period to an eighth of 8 s and records home as 3 s;
   `T/mandala:41` and EC:186 give home **3.5 s** ("the spec sheet rounds home to 3s"), and laws 7/8 say every place moves at its own
   tempo. The Foundation (12 Oct, line 40) lifts "one 8 s breath clock". → Restore Design's periods unsnapped; keep one *epoch*, not one
   *beat*.
2. **rk.js hold completes instantly on Enter/Space** (ST/*:`h.addEventListener('keydown' … done())`, e.g. ST/mandala:526), contradicting
   law 16 / SK:68 ("Enter/Space held as long; key repeat ignored"). The studio and brand-home builds all carry this. Any SwiftUI hold
   must follow law 16, not rk.js.
3. **Studio builds load no fonts** (no `<link>` in any ST/*), so the token proofs render in system/Georgia fallbacks — they prove
   colours and contrast, not the languages' type. The app must bundle 16 faces (B2) or the languages flatten again.
4. **Brand-home build uses a stale rk.css** (BH:333, 396–397) — the evidence image shows illegible dark-on-dark deep-band stat labels
   and a collapsed `.stat.rule`; studio builds carry the fix (ST/mandala:359–360). Lesson: measure every label on every world's deepest
   surface (the app's 33 rooms × deep grounds especially).
5. **The harness double-loads dc.js** (inline script + rk.js hook loading `harness/dc.js`, which does not exist beside `builds/`) — a
   404 and possible double mount; review-only, no app impact.
6. **"Light, never locks" vs the app's own gates.** Law 14 favours light; SK:127 keeps "a real gate … where locking is the meaning";
   his rulings keep Leela ≥3 days and dissolutions once a day (Foundation:44). Leela's board and the dissolutions are "locking as
   meaning" — they stay. Membranes and the stillness gate are designed gates — they stay.
7. **"One memory store on the device" (law 26) vs "App Activity is the single ledger"** (floor). The app's ledger is Airtable App
   Activity (off-device), so law 26's "nothing leaves the device until a signed-in layer exists" doesn't fit; the floor wins. *forget me*
   would need a ruling (practice data is "never lost").
8. **Live model / generated voice elements** (answering panel EC:1347; field gathers later EC:2644; staged answer EC:568; drawing
   from outside EC:1242; witness reflections) collide with "no generated voice". Carried here only as authored/his-own-words forms;
   generated forms → Feed or nowhere.
9. **Status-as-light opacities and "nothing scores him".** Mandala's mapped/exploring/active/embodied (.22/.44/.76/1) are labels of
   relationship, not grades — fine as light; but the Memory Layer's petal counts and bloom meters were left behind (EC:2747) and must stay
   out.
10. **Chassis B hues for Body 10, Mind 9, Tree 7 are "seeded — ratify per build"** (WO:31–33); Tree names "TO RATIFY against
    tree-of-life". The 33 world (chakra chassis covers only Energy 7) needs these 26 hues ruled; also Chakra 32 (Ego Reveal / Gaia)
    must keep its words sealed (ruling 2) — its room should be light without text.
11. **Tree 7 names in worlds.md (bindu · shadow · armin · maya · aatma · pointe · lalita)** vs the app's ruling Axis 18 / Crow 19 (both
    Body 10 in WO:31) — consistent; but the register's chakra numbering must be checked against these system names (05/06 own this).
12. **Seed syllables.** EC:1132, 1574 and §7 (2746) forbid bīja near the yantra and per-being drones derived from one; the floor here
    says "nothing invented (bījas, baked text verbatim)". Fonts.swift notes "`MandalaCanvasLayer` resolves its seat names, **bīja** and
    enclosure titles inside a Canvas" — a direct conflict with the corpus guard "no seed syllable is ever set in or near the figure".

15. **S12–S17 have no proof build** (SK:125; SH:160,180,204,226,246,267) — every app world built on them is the first proof; the parity

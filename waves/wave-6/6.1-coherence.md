## 3 · Coherence

### The Mandala's three quiet controls
**Sound (♪), the Veil's handle (the peek at the foot), and one "ways in" mark (✶).** Folded into the mark:
the zoom column (+ − ⤢), Wave 3's ✦ constellation and ☉ Road (`ChakraLayerOverlay` no longer draws
switches; it answers `ChakraLayerState.roadAsked / leaveRoadAsked`), and Slice C's ◌ mode door.

Touching ✶ opens a small quiet ring round itself (`Views/Mandala/WaysIn.swift`), sweeping from beside the
mark down into the glass, each way a 46 pt disc with one word: **the chakras** (constellation on/off),
**the road** (walk / leave), **the modes**, **her field** (Lalitā's field, via `PlayField.open()`),
**the climb** (`ChakraRingClimb` from the enclosure he has fallen into, or today's), **the whole** (fit).
A touch anywhere else closes it. VoiceOver: the mark is "Ways in" with named actions *Fall in*,
*Rise out*, *The whole yantra* (so nothing the removed zoom buttons did is lost to a voice); the
constellation and the Road keep the labels their switches carried. Pinch and double-tap still zoom.

### Found where they live
- **A game at its address:** `ChakraTeaching.games` now also gives a whole-ring chakra (Integration) its
  ring's games and the point its ninth ring's — Mandala Completion and the Twelve open from Integration's
  room. Seat and threshold games were already found there (A + E agree after the test fix).
- **A mode at its hour:** the Rite's opening (once each light/dark half of a day) writes one quiet word or
  two at its foot: at dawn *the night's dream* (Svapna) and the Sandhyā's *greet the sun*; at dusk *offer
  the day* (Pūjā) and the Sandhyā. A mode opens alone (`ModeHost`), the Sandhyā through the shell's one
  door into play. Nothing on the open day or at night.
- **Lalitā's field is the one place everything is gathered:** all 36 games, and at its foot *the modes*
  and *the road* (`MandalaWays.ask(.road)` turns the shell to the Mandala, rises out of the source if he is
  in it, and begins the Road; taken once).
- **Removed duplicates:** the four extra Mandala controls above. The modes door stays a door (reached from
  the ways, the field and the hour), not a fifth control.

### One sound at a time
Every engine now asks `SoundConductor`: ring voices (`.field`), room carrier and climb grounds (`.room`),
games' synth (`.play`), bīja / chakra-practice tones / threshold moments / the Rite's struck tones through
`BijaSoundService` (`.bija`), the bells (`.bell`), the Om (the floor). **New:** the heart register
(`.heart`, a bed in front of all — he chose to sing; `start` adopts it, `stop` releases it, its render
follows the conductor's gain) and his own kept voice played back (`.voice`, an accent: the bed steps back
while he hears himself). `RingAudioService.ringBija/ringChime` and stepped notes route through
`BijaSoundService` and so already strike. `HomeSoundService.strike` is the room's own voice inside its bed.

  - Title, type, games tags and "about" link were read for all 352.

**How to read "captured?"**
- **yes**: built, with the file or wave.
- **partial**: some of it is built; the ledger names the missing part.
- **no**: not present in code.
- **law**: a design law rather than a feature (see Part 3).
- **n/a**: bears on the topic but belongs to Feed, Field, Being, Learning or Money, not to Mandala.

## 3 · Laws the Codex sets that are not yet honoured, or are contradicted

Honoured already, so not repeated here: never measure him; no streaks on screen; no generated voice for her; two Recognition lines verbatim; position is identity; one ledger; telling recedes energy (nothing shares or posts); dormancy is never failure.

1. **"What is above creation cannot be attained by action."** (C-1307; also C-1161, C-1197, C-1260)
   - *Contradicted in part.* The Bindu is reached by **tap → Mauna** and **hold → Praveśa** (W2 §8), which are actions, and the climb rises *toward* it.
   - The Codex wants the centre *shown*: it arrives when he stops, and the summit is empty.
   - The fix: Mauna and Praveśa begin from stillness (no touch for one breath), not from a tap. Let the Bindu open *to him*.

2. **Energy recedes when focused on or told, even in anticipation** (C-1195, C-1047, C-1284)
   - *At risk.* Three things tilt toward "focus on her energy":
     - the Wager ("watch for her today… did she appear?");
     - the somatic way in;
     - the seal's "call her back".
   - The Wager is an act of watching. By C-1195 it may make her recede.
   - Keep it, but frame it as catching her in passing, never as watching for her. Its question should arrive later and lightly (it already waits until 17:00).

3. **"The system holds the door open and never the watch."** (C-1355)
   - *Contradicted in places:*
     - The seal is a 2.4 s timed hold (that one is fine: it is a gesture).
     - Mauna draws captions at 7, 14 and 21 seconds (W2 §8). These are a clock on his silence, even without the digit.
     - The Water Level game holds for "12 s" and Fire for "60 s" (W4 table). These are watches, though they are never shown.
   - Mauna's captions should follow his stillness, not elapsed seconds.

4. **Never tell him he is doing something wrong** (C-1357, C-1102, C-1091)
   - *At risk in Play.* Failure is present-tense physics: "the flame gutters, the door dims", and "a hard press dims" the Door (W4).
   - That is within the law as written. But the Codex wound is specifically about being told "wrong".
   - A dimming door is a correction aimed at *him*. Prefer physics that simply does not open over physics that reacts to his "error".

5. **Specialness collapses; everything ordinary is special** (C-1284)
   - *At risk:* "the core light for a Śakti he has stood with … *she was expecting you*" (W2 §1) and "met" halos (W1 A) mark some seats as more lit than others.
   - That is a description of the place, which the charter allows. But by C-1284 it divides the 102 into special and not.
   - Consider letting every seat carry the core light, with only *today's* crown distinct.

6. **Gratitude is felt, not done** (C-1159, C-1272)
   - Recognition is one tap ("I feel her") and is honoured.
   - But `Played`, `Wager Resolved`, `Portrait Drawn`, `State Set Down` and `Siddhi Received` all turn felt moments into logged acts.
   - Storage may count. The law holds as long as nothing on screen reflects these back as *done*. The Sakshi close names "who he stopped for", which edges toward a done-list.
   - Its "and others" guard (W3) is the right instinct. Keep it to presence, never to tasks.

7. **Never ask for a repeat of a peak** (C-1169, C-1033)
   - Grace (W3) reads the ledger back ("three nights ago you finished a door…").
   - Reading back a peak invites its repetition and the wanting of a state. Grace should never surface a peak moment as something to return to.
   - Also: the gift picks "her Appreciation Phrase", not his past peak. That is correct.

8. **Inhabit the gaps; don't fill them** (C-1348, C-1297)
   - *Contradicted by the year ring* (W5 §4): beads are "spaced evenly round the whole rim … so no day is ever an empty slot".
   - This was built to avoid debt, but it erases gaps. The Codex wants gaps to be inhabitable, not hidden.
   - A ring that shows silence as space, never as a missing bead, honours both.

9. **No wrong path; dark is not shamed** (C-1315, C-1302, C-1119)
   - Honoured on the Mandala (time states).
   - *At risk on the Veil*: "veiled" names for rings 8–9 (W1 A) can read as forbidden.
   - The Veil's own reasoning (read from where he stands) is sound. Keep the words "ahead" and "further in" and avoid "veiled" in VoiceOver.

10. **Feeling-level, not talking-level** (C-1177; also C-1305 "awareness is space")
    - The app adds many words: the room's words, weather sentences, the Sakshi close, grace, Gates captions, the Homecoming lines.
    - Each is authored and verbatim from his rows, which honours law 8. But C-1177 says the universe "works on the feeling level, not on the talking level".
    - A felt-only mode, where words come only on request (Legibility already floors them), would honour it.

11. **Low states are doors, not failures; don't reflect in a low** (C-1002, C-1120, C-1154)
    - *Not honoured* because no surface knows a low exists.
    - Grace and gift are blind to his state, so they may arrive as "reflection" at the worst moment.
    - The low-state door (Part 2 C5) answers this. So would "Let her arrive" respecting a self-declared low.

12. **Gains, never renunciations** (C-1230, C-1232)
    - Gates of Release asks him to "set down" a Mental State.
    - The Codex warns that giving up creates separateness that spreads.
    - Frame each gate as *receiving* on both crossings (inward: the space where the state was; outward: the Siddhi).

13. **One audio at a time** (C-1350)
    - Four engines and two session categories (`.ambient` / `.playback`) are toggled by different services (W2 §6, W3 notes, W4 §6).
    - He named this exact failure in Field.

14. **The mark is in the architecture, not in the claiming of it** (C-1338)
    - Honoured: the app never names him, a level or a title.
    - Watch the ARRIVAL Embodiment line "level 4 of 4", which is still open. By C-1338 and C-1284 it should go.

15. **Light cannot be measured, only witnessed** (C-1286)
    - *Contradicted in code vocabulary only.* The look checks pin brightness ratios (e.g. "Rite ≥ 2.6×", W1 B tests).

    - But the brightness of *his* felt light (felt halo vs unfelt) is a two-state measure of her by his acts. Keep it binary, as built, never graded.

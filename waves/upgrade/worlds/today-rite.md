## 1 · The world

### What it is

Today is the day's own world: the one Śakti who presides, met once a day or many times, in a space shaped by her element, lit by the hour, framed by the moon's Nityā, held by his own words. It is the first door from the Bindu (ruling 1), never the launch screen.

- **C-1303** — "Stop. Breathe. Remember." (07:830-833)

### The full experience

**Arriving.**
1. The Bindu stands in its met or unmet weather (Home world). Its first door is Today.
2. Touching Today starts **the blink crossing** (#2). Lids close to the seam over 0.66 s, the seam glints in today's colour, and the Rite opens at 780 ms. With sound on, the opening bell sounds once per practice day: 272.2 Hz, 5.5 s (#65).
3. **Station 1 · Arrival** — the first pane of Today's vertical space of six stations (#31):
   - A **threshold card** (#3) in one of five weighted kinds, never the same kind twice in a row. A touch passes it.
   - **One breath** (#4): a 5.5 s ring with four ticks, or a 5 s press-and-hold that also unlocks sound and the wake lock. Under it, pure air: a filtered-noise whoosh on a 4 s cycle. The breath arc follows the day's element (#7). On the first three entries ever, the **pre-roll** carrier comes first, alone at 0.020 (#5).
   - **The hour's call**:
     - at dawn: "Open the eyes before the world opens them for you."
     - at dusk: "What you carried today may be offered here."
   - **"Stop. Breathe. Remember."**, typed at about 250 ms per word.
   - **The Sacred Pause**, three breaths, with his words verbatim (#6). The circle's size is the day's place in the **102-day year**, felt as light (#11).
   - Optional gestures: **standing words** (mind · body · spirit · now) as four stars (#60), **your colour today** (#61), and **drift words** for "where are you?" (#62).
   - Ruling 16: this opening plays on every entry from the Bindu. A back-return skips it.

**Being there.**

4. **Station 2 · Summons.** Who presides and in what mood:
   - The **celestial strip**: moon glyph, "Śukla Ekādaśī · Nīlapatākā ›" (#21). It opens **the Nityā's sheet**, which rises 40 px from scale .96 over 1.1 s (#22).
   - **The day's line in three persons**, resolving word by word out of the dark (#34).
   - The **seed of the day**, typing itself at the day's tempo, with the panel verb and seed of today's chakra above it (#36).
   - **Value · Mantra · Role** as three words (#59).
   - **Whose day it is** (#69): a lead-time mark, or a name on the day itself.
   - The register of the hour, from his Routines (#68).
5. **Station 3 · Meeting.** The Living Rite face (#12–#25).
   - A body cue comes first (#38).
   - Her element picks one of **six plans**, so the screen's structure changes with her, not only its colour:
     - fire rises
     - water settles
     - air drifts and leans
     - ether becomes her name as the whole backdrop
     - earth grounds on a wide low sigil
     - light holds her name inside a dominant sigil
   - Her kp sets the mood bits: flip, spin, scale .86–1.26, name tier, gradient angle (#13).
   - Her palette comes from one hue (#9) and is relit by the hour (#10).
   - The backdrop is drawn layer for layer and breathes at the element's rate (#14). The bīja is a 280 px texture at α .07 (#16), the sigil spins at 140 + (hue mod 60) s (#15), and 16 motes move in the element's way (#17).
   - Nine beats bring the blocks in, from .10 to 1.25 s, and the sigil settles at 2.6 s (#18).
   - Her name is a door to Her Presence (#19, #20).
   - At the foot: the **grace line** (#26), or rarely **his gratitude** (#27); the **"I feel her" pill**, breathing at her ring's rate with a heartbeat haptic in rings 4–8 (#23, #24); "{kp} of 102 · bīja {x}" with the nine-ring mark (#25); and the doors of the hour (#28).
6. **"I feel her" → Recognition** (#52–#54, #86):
   - A full-screen takeover in her nature.
   - At 2 600 ms: "she was felt here · 9:41 AM". At 4 300 ms: "and she felt you back · 9:41:03", as two sines converge to 136.1 Hz (#53).
   - At 5 400 ms the note prompt appears and recedes as he writes.
   - The ceremony collapses in 0.62 s into his Portrait point.
   - Ruling 16: the closing stations follow every recognition.
7. **Station 4 · Being-with.** The practice of the day:
   - **The Reading**: her baked text sits dark on the page, and each touch lights one paragraph. The archetype voices gather; who speaks in full is drawn from the day (#37).
   - **The breath of the day**, by verb (#39), under the binaural arc (#40), opened only by stillness (#41) with the wake lock held (#42).
   - **The day's mudrā**, three breaths (#44).
   - The element's gesture (#80).
   - **Rām japa** when kp 42 presides (#50).
   - The Temple position (#51), verses (#79), and the **carried practices** shelf (#29, #30, #45–#49).
8. **Station 5 · Offering**:
   - Her Appreciation Phrase for 6 s.
   - **A word asked without a box**, or spoken (#55).
   - **The carve**, "you" → "I" on stone.
   - **One struck bowl**, then "The room has changed." (#56).
9. **Station 6 · Carry**:
   - Her somatic line "into your day" for 8 s.
   - The **trikona close** of Knowledge, Will and Action, whose centre lights (#57).
   - The **wager** (#58). It ends outward.

**Dawn and dusk** (#64). At dawn the opening carries the Śṛṣṭi call and the Svapna door, and the Prātaḥ Sandhyā is offered facing east. At dusk it carries the Saṃhāra offering and the Pūjā door, and the Sāyam Sandhyā faces west. Sandhyā times come from the real sun at home (Ephemeris, ±24 min). On festival days the dusk station lights eight lamps (#70).

**The low-state door** (#63). A line, "I am low", always sits top-leading on every station. It opens his own words, one at a time, and asks no question.

**Leaving.**
- **Closing the day** (#66), from 21:00 or by choice:
  - One witnessing sentence from what was walked, ending "It trusts you."
  - The motes of what he took up today orbit the point, uncounted.
  - The day's six petals light by register.
  - The closing bell sounds: 136.1 Hz, 7 s.
  - The Pūjā door releases the motes into the point (#67).
- **Other ways out**:
  - the point (home)
  - the menu
  - Her Presence (name)
  - her room through the Rite of Entering (#73)
  - the Climb at her light (#74)
  - the Modes (the hour's mode)
  - Time (the Nityā and tithi world, the year ring)
  - the Well (low-state letters)
  - Back returns to exactly the station and breath phase left (#75).

- **Bells.**
  - Opening bell: 272.2 Hz, 5.5 s at .2.
  - Closing bell: 136.1 Hz, 7 s at .22.
  - The tapped bell is the singing bowl: partials 1 / 2.76 / 5.4 / 8.93, 5.5 s (#48, #65).
- **Sealing bowl.** 220 Hz, 11 s, with the bed ducking to .006 (#56).
- **Ink tone.** 174 Hz through a 600 Hz low-pass while he writes.
- **Felt-back.** 132 + 140 Hz converge to 136.1 Hz (#53).
- **Bīja tone.** The pentatonic synth (#53). It is a tone, never a voice.
- **Breath sessions.** Each brings its own carrier and beat (#39).
- **The Rite's binaural arc.** It runs 8 → 6 → 4.5 → 4.0 at Recognition → 7.5 → 10 Hz, and freezes in stillness (#40).
- **No songs in Today.** A song's drone plays only during its dance. The Ember sitting is in the Well.
- **Sound gate.** Sound is unlocked by the opening breath's hold (#4). No generated voice anywhere.

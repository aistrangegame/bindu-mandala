## 1 · The worlds

### 1.1 The Modes — what it is

**In his words (Codex §III, 03:111-122).** "Eight modes of engagement — not screens, but practices. Each has its own duration, posture, intention. The mode is determined by the moment, not a menu." The eight are:

- दर्शन **Darśana**: "To be with the yantra without doing anything. The breath finds it. The eye softens. The mind comes home."
- प्रत्यभिज्ञा **Recognition**: "A shakti has just arrived in life. You name her, you receive her, she receives you."
- साधना **Sādhana**: "The Khaḍgamāla — sword-garland — sequential walking through all 102 names with their bījas."
- स्मृति **Smṛti**: "The constellation of past recognitions. When have you been here? With whom? The yantra remembers."
- मौन **Mauna**: "The undifferentiated point. All of her, here, always. The place no name reaches. Rest."
- स्वप्न **Svapna**: "What arrived in the night? Which shakti walked through the dream?"
- पूजा **Pūjā**: "End of day: the catches of the day witnessed together. A small offering. The day given back."
- सङ्घ **Saṅgha**: "the field" (C-1111 adds that play mode is legitimate: modes come and go without guilt).

**In Design's words (Phase IV, `modes.jsx` header).** "Each mode is its own atmosphere. Not a feature, a practice." On Saṅgha, Phase IV reframes the Codex: "The field is all you. There are no strangers in it. Two layers: INNER — the 102 shaktis as your living community. OUTER — the people you love as carriers of specific shaktis."

**What it becomes.** The Modes is a world of its own, entered through the point. Its home is a **ring of eight lights round the point** (M01). The eight modes sit on the inner ring. The practice instruments that belong to the Modes sit on an outer ring:

- **Prāṇa**: the eleven breath sessions of the Akash library (M42–M48).
- **Nāda**: the planetary tones, the beat offered alone, and the Lab (M49–M51).
- The open sitting with the seven forms of time (M53).
- What is carried for life: twenty portable practices (M54).
- The Bell (M55), Mudrā (M56) and the senses (M57).
- Learning's practices (M58), the heart register (M59), ways in by how he feels (M60), the body as a way in (M62).
- The steady horizon (M65).

Each mode keeps its Design atmosphere and Design's exact lines. Each can also be opened by its Codex gesture from Home or the Mandala (M03), as extra doors beside the menu.

#### Arriving

Ways in:

- the menu's Modes point
- a Codex gesture on Home (the Bindu) or on the Mandala
- the hour's way from Today (dawn → Svapna, dusk → Pūjā)
- a sandhyā or the evening in Time
- the Catch from anywhere (G9)

Every crossing travels through the point. The point blooms from the touch over 0.9 s in the tint of the mode entered (hsl(h 55% 68%) on the void #020208), and the ring settles out of the bloom.

When he arrives on the ring itself, the lit mode is the one the moment asks for (M02), and the rest sit at .45:

- first open of the day, before solar noon: Svapna
- inside a sandhyā (Ephemeris, ±24 min): the sandhyā door in Time
- after the real sunset: Pūjā
- otherwise: Darśana

Every mode stays open at every hour. Behind both rings, the 102 names breathe at .08: this is ambient Saṅgha (M41).

#### Being there, mode by mode

Each mode is described second by second here. The numbers are in §2 and §3.

- **Darśana** (M06–M09). He raises the phone upright and holds it still. Touch does nothing.
  - At 3 steady seconds the ground darkens from #0A0608, the yantra stands at 280 pt with 6 motes, and "hold still · she wakes" appears.
  - At 30 s the yantra grows to 320 and 10 motes drift. The bījas of the present Śaktis surface on a circle at .62 of the yantra, each looping 4 s and staggered .35 s. "beholding · 30s" appears.
  - At 2 minutes the yantra is 360 with 14 motes. Every piece of chrome is gone, a 540 pt radiance breathes on 6 s, and the label is "∞".
  - If he moves, the radiance loosens, but a line that has appeared stays.
- **The Catch** (M10–M11). A long-press anywhere (0.8 s) raises a 360 pt sheet over the faint mandala. The sheet carries today's Śakti and a name line he can swipe across all 102.
  - He taps the 68 px circle. Its 18 px gold centre fills over 1.2 s and the caption reads "she is named".
  - About 1.5 s later "and she felt you back" arrives in gold, and her petal glows 60 px behind the sheet.
  - The footer reads "LOGGED · {time}", with no tally. The catch writes one ledger row.
- **Sādhana** (M12–M19).
  - **Saṅkalpa:** "I begin the garland / not to gain anything, / but to remember / what I have always been." Begin sits beside a sound switch that is **off by default**.
  - **Stations:** one Śakti at a time, at 16.5 s per name (a tap moves on), with nine ring dots and the garland line filled to her kp out of 102.
  - **Transitions:** at each ring the screen reads "DESCENDING" with the next enclosure's name in gold and its two-line gloss, and waits for "tap to continue".
  - **Bindu:** "Lalitā" · "aiṁ klīṁ sauḥ" · "One hundred and two have been said. / What remains is what was always there."
  - **Visarjana:** "What I have received, / I release." · "The garland is offered back / to the one who wove it. / The 102 return to the One." · "tap to close · she remains".
  - With sound on, each name that has a bīja sounds it. Names without a bīja stay silent.
- **Smṛti** (M20–M25). Every felt seat is one light. Seats felt on the same day are threaded in gold at .16, and the newest twinkle. "where she has been most alive".
  - Tapping a star (or arriving by G3 on a ring) opens one Śakti's cluster with her last-caught line.
  - The scrubber replays the sky day by day from the first ledger day, labelled only by dates.

- **Mauna** (M26–M28). The ground is #020001 with an 84 px bindu breathing on 6 s, Ring 2 names on r158 and Ring 3 names on r110. Each further ring he has walked adds an inner orbital and grows the bindu. Captions arrive by steady seconds:

- **Svapna** (M29–M33). This is built **as designed: he tells the dream.**
  - Pre-dawn peach light, 12 motes, and "Who walked through / last night?" with two choices: "I felt someone" or "The night was still".
  - With "I felt someone" he taps the petals and seats of who walked through. Each glows (30 px, pulse 3 s). Holding a mark adds a note.
  - Then the mic: "tell the dream while it is still here". A 42-bar waveform follows his own voice and his words appear as he speaks, or he writes instead ("OR WRITE").
  - Under it all, the record's walkers from the last practice day drift faintly as a second layer. The record keeps him company; it never answers for him.

- **Pūjā** (M34–M37). After sunset the screen wears the candle chassis (#100A08, gold #C9A84C) with a sunset glow at .12.
  - Today's catches stand as cluster-coloured dots round the point, then as rows of time · name.

  - "The day is received. / Tomorrow opens again." · "SLEEP WITH THE FIELD". The closing bell sounds at 136.1 Hz if sound is on.
- **Saṅgha** (M38–M41). **Restored.**
  - **Inner:** the 102 as named orbitals breathing 5–7 s. "One hundred and two living forces. / This is your assembly."
  - **Outer:** the souls of the Relationship Field as breathing nodes, with dashed threads to the seats they carry: Gaia → Ahaṅkārākarṣiṇī kp 31, Ashrey → Nāmākarṣiṇī kp 40, Ram → Ātmākarṣiṇī kp 42. "The people you love / are her wearing human faces."

### 1.2 Time — what it is

**In Design's words (Phase V, `temporal.jsx`).**

- "The yantra is alive in time. Hour, tithi, nakshatra, moon, season, year."
- Six instruments: "Cosmic Now · Lunar Yantra · Sandhya · Seasonal · Great Wheel · Tithi". The Data & Time Contract closes: "Every ring-world, lunar phase, seasonal yantra, and sandhyā is the live home under a different sky … Time and atmosphere modulate the visual — they do not remove the instrument."
- From the Codex's five dimensions: "The yantra breathes with cosmic time. Different shaktis ascend at different hours, moons, seasons. Dawn-yantra is not dusk-yantra."
- The Living Rite's governing idea: "Time is the medium."

**In his words.**

- C-1260 on cadence: "every day you practice, you do your daily sadhana, you do your weekly sadhana, you do your monthly, yearly".
- C-1353 on the gift that comes at a random hour: "variability, the randomness … they come bearing a gift".
- C-1291: "doesn't have a beginning, and doesn't have an end".

**What it becomes.** Time is the world that owns the real sky. The engine is already built and verified against USNO: Ephemeris, Panchanga and LunarPhaseService. That engine moves **out of the Portrait** (where the five clocks hid behind the Point door, PortraitMandalaView:191-228) **into Time** as TimeService (T02), which every world reads. Time's own space is one sky: the live yantra under the present sky state, slowly turning, with the schematic yantra turning once every 240 s far behind.

#### Arriving

Ways in:

- the menu's Time point
- the celestial strip in Today (tap the moon line)
- the widget (T29)
- the Bindu's sandhyā door when a junction is open
- the moon or tithi named in any world

He crosses through the point, which blooms in deep indigo #0B0A18 over 0.9 s. On arrival the sky's ambient rises by min(.92, .30 + .62·(1 − e^(−minutes/40))): this is "a dawn that moved while you were away" (T25). The glow breathes on the wall clock's 8 s phase (T24).

#### Being there: the Cosmic Now and its doors

The ground state is **the Cosmic Now** (T04–T05), matching frame F03 without its label collision. Five wheels stand round a 14 px crimson bindu that breathes 13 ↔ 16 over 5 s:

| wheel | radius | ticks | colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| hour | r158 | 24 | #C9963F |
| tithi | r130 | 30 (bright half #E8C97A, dark half at .45) | #E8C97A |
| nakṣatra | r102 | 27 | #D4A067 |
| ṛtu | r74 | 6 | #D4A017 |
| sun (rāśi) | r48 | 12 | #C45050 |

Each wheel has one lit bead. The names live only in the legend below. Then come "THIS MOMENT", e.g. "Sandhyā · the evening junction. / Anurādhā watches the moon waxing.", and the moon's own line: "ŚUKLA PAKṢA · DAY 13 · THE MOON IS 78% FULL".

The bindu's light is the day's place in the year of 102 (T22). While he moves, the wheels tremble and then settle.

Each wheel is a door:

- **The hour wheel → the Day.** The three sandhyās and the day's stations (T11, T17).
  - At a junction (one muhūrta, ±24 min round sunrise, solar noon and sunset) the sky becomes that sandhyā's five-stop gradient.
  - Prātaḥ · Brāhmī (kp 11): "Open the eyes / before the world opens them for you." · "three breaths · facing east".
  - Madhyāhna · Vaiṣṇavī (kp 14), the one light-ground screen: "The sun has nothing to hide. / Neither do you." · "one minute · still upright".
  - Sāyam · Rudrāṇī (Māheśvarī, kp 12): "What you carried today / you may set down here." · "three breaths · facing west".
  - "begin sandhyā" is a 1.1 s hold. The breaths follow the Pace form, the yantra's east gate turns to true east, and the Gāyatrī may follow one line per screen. The opening bell sounds at dawn and the closing bell at dusk.
  - Outside the junctions the Day shows the stations (dawn · morning · noon · evening · night, with one pause each) coloured by his Routines' register, and the night and dawn carried practices (T21).
- **The tithi wheel → the Moon.**
  - The Lunar Yantra in its live atmosphere (T07): Amāvasyā's 90 px rising bindu, "when the moon is hidden, / the Bindu rises." … Kṛṣṇa's "she returns to seed." Every recognition is plotted at its tithi on an inner ring.
  - Then **Today's Tithi** (T08–T10): the moon at its real illumination, the tithi named truly (Kṛṣṇa Pratipadā for tithi 16), and "SHE WEARS TODAY THE FORM OF" over the Nityā in 46 pt gold.
  - On Pūrṇimā: "the full moon belongs to Lalitā, seated in the Bindu, from whom the whole yantra breathes."
  - Her quality, bīja and dhyāna come from the Mandala table when present. When absent, nothing is drawn.
- **The nakṣatra wheel** opens the nakṣatra line of THIS MOMENT, with the name and its place among the 27.
- **The ṛtu wheel → the Seasons** (T13). Four atmospheres, from Vasanta's "after the long silence, / the field begins to remember itself." to Hemanta's "she is in the seed now. / you keep her warm inside you." The six ṛtus are named beneath, and the season is hemisphere-aware.
- **The sun wheel, or pinch out from the point → the Year.**
  - **The Great Wheel** (T14): 365 rays lit by how often she was met that day, four season sectors, and the year's real full moons at r168. Today pulses about 60 times a minute. Swiping moves between years; tapping a day opens its catches; pinching nests day → fortnight → four months → year.
  - Round it, **the special days** (T15): Act lights in each person's hue, Notice days hollow, Lead Time brightening their marks days ahead, and his own foundation days as a gold seam.
  - **Festivals and observances** (T16): Diwali's arc of the sun with eight lamps lit from dusk, Uposatha's four moon days, and the others.
  - Holding over the years plays **the Signal** (T23): his years appear one by one, and the drone resolves to 136.1 Hz at now.
  - Opt-in **long arcs** begin here (T26): forty days up the spine, a moon, a season.

#### Leaving

- The point returns home.
- Back climbs from an instrument to the Cosmic Now, then to where he came from.
- A day on the Great Wheel opens Smṛti or Memory. A special-day mark opens the person in the Relationship Field.
- A sandhyā's Śakti opens her room in Her Home. The Nityā sheet's close returns to the Moon.

#### Visual language and sound

The house mandala is relit by the hour, with the three junction skies.

- **Linen** is the morning page (#F6EFE2 / #25201A / ember #B8593A). **Candle** is dusk.
- Cormorant and Roman-numeral tracked caps are used at the crossing; the date is written DD · MM · YYYY in numerals.
- The day's colour journeys by shortest-hue interpolation (hold 30 / move 40 / hold 30).
- The instruments' glyphs come from the tiles by kind of time.

Sound is silent by default. The bells sound at the junctions, and the Signal's drone resolves to 136.1 Hz. Every number Time shows is about the sky (tithi, illumination, ṛtu), never about him.

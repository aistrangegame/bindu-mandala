### 1.1 What it is, in his words and Design's

The 33 is the braid: thirty-three rooms of energy, each crossed into, each walked by five threads (the story told by a moving point, the practice, the wake-up, the dance, the gate), joined into one Road that begins and ends at the point.

### 1.2 The space

The 33 is its own world (foundation: each world is its own space, nothing laid over another). It no longer lives on the Mandala (§2 row 1). It has three scales, joined by one camera and the moving point:

1. **The field of the 33** (the world's arrival space, §2 row 2). Ground night `#0C0A12`. A faint Śrī Yantra breathes beneath everything in this world (4 apex-up triangles, 5 apex-down, bindu r 1.4, circles r 88 and r 90; opacity .035 → .058 and scale 1 → 1.025 over 14 s; gold light poured from above, radial `rgba(201,168,76,.14)`; grain; `14-aistrangegame.md:745-748`). 2. **The Road** (§2 row 49). The camera travels the 33 in nine stages, centre → perimeter → back to the point. The moving point leads; the camera follows. A horizontal carousel of station cards (§2 row 3) is the Road's hand-held form.
3. **The room** (§2 rows 8–20). Full screen, its own ground, its own colour, its own geometry, its own tone. Crossed into by the rite, left by a held withdrawal.

### 1.3 Arriving (second by second)

From any world, the always-available menu's constellation has a point for the 33; touching it, or any door on the three threads (a chakra number, a Śakti's room door, the day), runs the **crossing through the point**: the colour flood (overlay 0 → .92 over .70 s ease-in, the destination glyph scaling ×11, navigation at 700 ms; `13-rupakarshini.md:533-534`) in the colour of the world entered. For the 33 world that colour is the hex of the room being entered, or, for the field, gold `#C9A84C`.

- **Into the field.** 0–1.4 s: atmosphere wash in the last-focused chakra's colour at opacity .18, transitioning 1.4 s (`14-aistrangegame.md:749`). The yantra beneath fades up to .035. The orbs light in body order, bottom to top, 0.8 s per segment as the hairline draws down and doors are sewn on (`13-rupakarshini.md:395`). The SilenceEngine stays at gain 0 (it sounds only inside rooms).
- **Into a room.** The room's door (a 52 px glyph disc of the destination; §2 row 4) leans the world toward the room's colour (.55 over .55 s), the piece fades to .2, navigation at 520 ms. Then the room shell runs: threshold card → rite → arrival (§1.4).

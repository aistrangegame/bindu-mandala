**Its place in the app.** The Climb is world 7 of the fifteen. It is one continuous vertical 3D instrument. The nine āvaraṇas are stacked up the body as nine weathers: Feet, Pelvis, Navel, Heart, Throat, Forehead, Crown, Above crown and Totality, 30 units apart, TOP 240. All 102 Śaktis stand at their altitude as points of light that look alike.

Down the left edge runs the spine. The ten body chakras live there, in the order the body moves through them: Soles 10, Forge 11, Prāṇa 12, Hands 13, Pulse 14, Sonar 15, Echo 16, Vision 17, **Axis 18**, **Crow 19** (ruling 3). Each body chakra carries its real instrument: press-and-hum, warmth, breath, rub, the beat, the word, the silence, the inner image, the blink, the shake-then-still.

Two **membranes** resist until he means it: **Shadow between Navel and Heart (3→4)** and **Armin between Forehead and Crown (6→7)**. At the top, a **stillness gate** opens onto an empty summit.

The Climb is its own world. Today it is launched as an overlay from the Mandala (G7-0211). That changes: it moves into its own place in the router.

### The experience, second by second

**Arriving** (§5 lists every door).

1. A door is taken: the menu's Climb point, the Mandala's ways, Today's "rise to her", a chakra room's "into the Nth āvaraṇa, on the climb", the Field's "rise through them", a kp/chakra/day thread, or search.
2. The crossing goes through the point (spine law). The point blooms from where he touched, in the Climb's colour: the gem of the world he will open in.
3. The boot veil *"the axis"* (12 px, .3em, uppercase, opacity .5) fades over 900 ms and is removed at 1000 ms (G1-0067).
4. The Climb opens at the altitude the thread names:
   - a Śakti (kp) opens at her ring, facing her light;
   - a chakra opens at its band;
   - Today opens at the presiding Śakti's āvaraṇa, facing her crown light;
   - the menu opens at the altitude he left, or at Feet the first time.
5. The first arrival of each day (Feet only) shows *"Everything begins as a point."* (Design's CMF Act 0, G5-0619). It rises 3 s after landing, focus-not-fade, and is gone at 9 s.

**Being there.**

- **The view.** The camera breathes slowly: eye `(sin(t·0.07)·0.5, climb+1.2, 9+cos(t·0.05)·0.4)`, looking 9 units ahead.
- **Drag up.** Drag up to rise. The world settles toward the target at `climb += (target−climb)×0.06` (0.035 under reduced motion).
- **Drag across.** Drag across to turn: yaw 0.0024 rad/pt, max π.
- **Weather.** The weather blends continuously as he rises: fog colour, density, swallow and the band's own clock (§2 #6–7). It never cuts.
- **The lights.** Every light breathes the same breath (§2 #9).
- **Words.** The world words sit high in the frame: kicker, name, material, weather sentence. The nearest light's line sits low.
- **The rails.** The right-edge rail names the nine regions; its current tick is labelled with the depth tier (THE RING / THE SEAT / FELT). The left-edge spine carries the energy nodes, the body lights' traces and the 33 vertebra ticks.
- **Chrome.** The chrome sinks after 3.8 s and thins with altitude.
- **Sound.** The ground voice crossfades nine ring voices by altitude. Her carrier and the withheld fifth play when a light is near. The Om (136.1 Hz) is always present under everything, and its floor rises with depth. The binaural beat narrows from 8 Hz at the Feet to 4 Hz at Totality.

**Going deeper.**

- **Enter her.** Touch her light. Three self-paced beats of the rite carry him in three surges of flight. Her fog swallows the Climb and the point becomes her room (§2 #16): the crossing into Her Home is the flight.
- **Feel her.** Long-press her light to feel her where she stands. The two Recognition lines appear and the rail tick reads FELT (§2 #17).
- **Body lights.**
  - Touch a body light and its partner glows, its dyad colours turn, and a thread runs to its energy twin on the spine.
  - Hold it (0.6 s) and its own gate instrument runs right there on the spine.
  - Tap its door and an arch rises into its chakra room (the-33).
- **Membranes.** Push through the Shadow and Armin membranes.
- **Summit.** Rise into Totality and stay still.

**Leaving.**

- The point (always visible, gold → ember by depth) goes home.
- The menu goes to any world.
- A light goes to Her Home. A body door goes to the 33.
- The rail's Today goes back to her crown.
- Back returns to exactly where he was. The Climb remembers its altitude and yaw.

### Sound

- **Ground.** Nine ring techniques, one tuning (Design's roots `[55, 61.74, 69.30, 73.42, 82.41, 92.50, 98, 110, 123.47]` Hz):
  - ring 1 drone;
  - ring 2 breath;

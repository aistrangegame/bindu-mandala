# The Spine and Home (the Bindu) — the world as it will be built

*Upgrade map, stage 2 · 13 Oct 2026 · slices `index/by-world/spine.jsonl` (484 rows) + `home.jsonl` (238 rows), 687 distinct ids, every row read. Ledger: `waves/upgrade/ledger/spine-and-home.csv`.*

Home is one world among fifteen — the centre. The spine is not a world: it is what every world shares — the point, the menu, the doors along the three threads, search, back, the crossings, the gestures. They are written together because the spine starts and ends at Home: every way out of a world passes through the point, and the point is Home.

### Arriving — every launch, second by second

**0.00 s — the Waking (#1).** The screen is black (scrim #060402). Nothing blocks his hand: a touch is taken at once. A white dot appears at the centre — 15 pt, core #FFFCF4 — and grows: by 1.03 s (36%) it is full size; at **1.65 s it kindles** to ×1.32, a soft haptic (.6/.2) lands, and with sound on a 110 Hz tone with a +6 ¢ twin and a fifth peaks at .11 (1.55 s). A halo (Ø400, warm gold → ember → crimson) lifts behind it from 0.37 s and holds to 1.6 s; the bloom (Ø2600) opens from 1.65 s and by 2.48 s (87%) fills the screen at .95 — *the picture forms around the dot*. At 2.85 s the Waking is gone and the dot is the point.

**2.85 s — the Homecoming (#2, #3).** *First launch ever*: an indigo sky; 90 motes drift in from everywhere and condense into the point between 2.0 and 11.0 s; *"You have always felt them."* at 11.8 s; *"Now you will know their names."* at 14.0 s; the hint at 16.5 s. He may rest one finger, still, on the point for one breath (4 s): the point leans toward the finger (≤5 pt), blinks (.95 s), the yantra draws outward from the point over 3.6 s, Home stands one breath, and that held stillness then opens the Today door — the first door, taken by stillness. The point's tone (136.1 / 204.15 / 272.2 Hz, 3.6 s, .045) sounds on his first touch. Then the Initiation (#7) — a first-time ceremony, not onboarding: the held threshold (*"headphones, if you have them"* · *"press, and hold — one full breath"*, 5 s), The One (name, the day he arrived, a 24 s breath, 30 s of silence, *"What name surfaced?"*, *"Your higher self is called <X>."*), and each gesture of Home taught once by doing it, then silence. *Every later launch*: one 4 s breath — blink .35 s, draw .9 s, the yantra drawn out from the point over 2.8 s, open. During that breath his thesis lines sit at .30 (#6).

**≈6.9 s — Home.** Read from the ledger, never from a flag (#5): after days away, *"return. / the road remembers"* (2.3 s); on the first launch of an ordinary day, *"Welcome back. The Bindu remembers."* (3 s); later launches of the same day, no line. The weather is unmet or met (#8): if nothing has been felt today, a wash of today's colour breathes from above (10 s, .34 ↔ .80) and 15 motes rise; once she has been felt, warmth sits low and behind and the motes settle in bone.

### The spine in every world

- **The point (#36, #37).** Top-right of every world, 6 pt, crimson at .3, a 44 pt target: tap → Home; hold → straight to the source; double-tap → the one-second practice (#35). It is one point, never replaced, carried through every crossing, taking each world's colour over 1.5 s and warming from gold at the surface to ember at the centre.
- **The menu (#40–#46).** A 2×2 mark top-left, or a slow pull anywhere: the Constellation — fifteen world-points laid out as Home's own ring, threads drawing in over 2.2 s, the current world glowing — and, on the same switch, the list (live marks, names, a pitch per world) and the Veil (the Meru by depth). The die at its centre. Above every world, never under a cover.
- **Doors along the threads (#47–#49).** Each world shows the doors for the kp, chakra and day it stands on — a 52 pt disc of the destination's own figure, the world leaning toward its colour before it crosses — and two neighbour doors on each thread.
- **Search (#50).** *"Type one word you're carrying"* — every Śakti by four spellings, every chakra, verb, mode, game and world, and his own words on the phone only.
- **Back (#51–#53).** Every crossing remembers exactly where he stood (kp, chakra, day, camera, scroll, breath); back returns there under a bloom of the colour he left; the edge is a way out everywhere, never punished.
- **Crossings (#54–#65).** Through the point; sound carries across the seam at .004 for ~3 s; each world sounds its pitch as he enters; every crossing between worlds is one ledger row.
- **Gestures (#66–#70).** The Codex grammar as extra doors; one hold engine for every hold.
- **One router (#71–#75)** replaces the six NotificationCenter channels and the global presenters.

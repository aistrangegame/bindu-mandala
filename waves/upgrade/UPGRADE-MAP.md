## How each wave is proven

Every wave passes all of these before it merges, and the ledger rows for that wave are filled with the evidence:

1. **Design numbers read from Design's own files** (not retyped) — a missing Design file fails.
2. **Frame parity** against F01–F40 (crop of the phone, layout within 3%, brightness band, hue within 15°, exact wording). Design is the floor: brighter passes, dimmer fails.
3. **Light, sound and timing measured** — rooms on a 64 × 64 luminance grid; audio rendered offline and checked to ~0.2 Hz; arrival beats and the Recognition timeline timed.
4. **The automated walk** — cold launch → every world by every route; one world on screen at a time; the point everywhere; back to where he was; three phone sizes, large type, reduced motion; a screenshot and layout dump at every step.
5. **The padakarshini gate** — every step lit, fits the screen, last step reached, 44 pt targets, holds need a real held press (a tap does nothing, early release drains).
6. **Every check born red** — it must fail on a planted defect before it counts.
7. **Run on Ashrey's Mac** in the simulator, screenshots into the ledger, then **his walk on Neev**, then an **honest report** of what was and wasn't checked.

# Wave 4 — Play

*Branch `wave/4-play`, built on `wave/1-light`. Waves 2, 3 and 5 are built in parallel.*

Lalitā at the Bindu is the play that knows itself. The arc of every visit is
**recognise → appreciate and dwell → play**, and this wave builds the last
movement: A Strange Game's real games, each a physical verb with a skill in it,
each at its address in the yantra, and all of them gathered again at the centre
as Lalitā's field.

## What is built

- `iOS/Bindu Mandala/Views/Play/` — sixteen games, one host, Lalitā's field, the
  instruments they are felt through (TouchSense, LightBody, the synth, the
  haptics), and the pure physics behind every skill.
- **At her address.** Every Śakti's Detail carries a play door between *be with
  her ›* and *I feel her*: the game at her seat where there is one, and her own
  bead in the 102-bead garland everywhere else. So every one of the 102 can be
  played with, and the door asks only her position.
- **At the centre.** Lalitā's source view gains *play in her field*; the field
  stands each game's light at its own ring, with the garland strung round it.
- **From anywhere.** `PlayField.open(_:)` (a notification the root answers) and
  `PlayField.host(_:atPosition:onClose:)` (a view to put in your own cover).
  Wave 3's Shadow threshold opens the Corridor with `PlayField.open(.corridor)`.
- **One ledger.** A played game is one App Activity row: `Activity Type`
  **Played**, `Gesture Source` **Play**, linked to her Shakti row (or the
  Avaraṇa row of the ring it was played in), `Duration (sec)` kept in storage
  and never shown. A game handed to her Recognition writes its own
  `Shakti Recognized` row with `Gesture Source` **Play**. Both options are born
  by `typecast: true`.

Codex · Phase I of VIII

The Living Yantra

An instrument for recognizing the Mother
through the 102 forces of the Sri Yantra

This is not an app. It is a temple compressed into glass — a living yantra you carry, breathe with, return to. Every interaction is a moment of recognition: of her, in her many forms, present in the texture of your day. The interface is not a wrapper around the practice. The interface is the practice.

What this document is

A codex — the architectural manual of the system before any screen is designed. It establishes the ontology so the design has somewhere to stand. Five dimensions. Eight modes. No menu. Nine ring-worlds, each with its own atmosphere. The home as a living state, not a screen. A phased path that lets the instrument emerge slowly, with care.

Read it slowly. Then we begin.

§ I · The Reframe

The yantra is the ontology.

A content app has tabs because its world is flat — items arranged in categories, each a peer to the next. A consciousness instrument cannot be flat. The structure of awareness is not peer; it is centered. Everything radiates from a center. So this app radiates from the yantra.

The Sri Yantra is not a feature inside the app. It is the world the app lives inside. It has presence, mood, time, depth, memory, and relationship. It is not the same yantra at dawn and dusk. It is not the same yantra after a hundred recognitions and zero. It is alive.

Which means the architecture is not a list of screens. The architecture is the set of dimensions through which you relate to the yantra. There are five of them.

§ II · The Five Dimensions

Five axes of relationship

The yantra is not a single object — it is a five-dimensional field. Each dimension is a way the instrument moves, and a way you move within it.

Depth

the inward axis

Nine avaraṇas as nine depths of consciousness. You move inward, not sideways. Each ring is its own world. You can rest at any depth.

Time

the cyclical axis

The yantra breathes with cosmic time. Different shaktis ascend at different hours, moons, seasons. Dawn-yantra is not dusk-yantra. The app pulls you into temporal awareness.

Memory

the accumulation axis

Every recognition leaves a luminous trace. Over months and years, the yantra becomes uniquely yours — bright where you have met her, mystery where you haven't.

Relationship

the radial axis

Shaktis cluster around the people in your life. Ram is Ātmā's frequency. Ashrey is Nāma. Gaia is Ahaṅkāra. The work of recognition is inseparable from the work of love.

Practice

the modal axis

Eight modes of engagement — not screens, but practices. Each has its own duration, posture, intention. The mode is determined by the moment, not a menu.

§ III · The Eight Modes

Eight ways of being with her

These are not features. They are practices. The yantra is the same; the way you meet it changes by the hour and by the heart.

दर्शन
Darśana — Beholding
1–10 min · hold phone vertical

To be with the yantra without doing anything. The breath finds it. The eye softens. The mind comes home.

प्रत्यभिज्ञा
Recognition — The Catch
~5 sec · long-press anywhere

A shakti has just arrived in life. You name her, you receive her, she receives you. The net deployed and retrieved.

साधना
Sādhana — The Recitation
20–30 min · pinch outward

The Khaḍgamāla — sword-garland — sequential walking through all 102 names with their bījas. The full ceremony.

स्मृति
Smṛti — The Memory
open · long-press a ring

The constellation of past recognitions. When have you been here? With whom? The yantra remembers.

मौन
Mauna — The Silence
open · tap the bindu

The undifferentiated point. All of her, here, always. The place no name reaches. Rest.

स्वप्न
Svapna — The Dream
3 min · morning opening

What arrived in the night? Which shakti walked through the dream? The morning has its own recognitions.

पूजा
Pūjā — The Offering
5 min · evening opening

End of day: the catches of the day witnessed together. A small offering. The day given back.

सङ्घ
Saṅgha — The Field
ambient · always on

Awareness — anonymous, soft — of other practitioners walking the same yantra. Not alone in this.

The mode is read from the moment. The moment determines the mode. There is no menu to choose from — only the gesture that fits.

§ IV · Navigation Vocabulary

There is no menu.

The yantra is the navigation. Every move is a movement of attention — inward, outward, around. Nine gestures form the entire grammar.

Tap a ring

descend

Enter that avaraṇa's world. The ring expands; the others fall away.

Tap the bindu

silence

Mauna. The undifferentiated point. All names gone.

Long-press a ring

remember

Smṛti. The constellation of every recognition you've made in this ring.

Pinch outward

recite

Sādhana. The Khaḍgamāla begins. All 102 names, sequential.

Hold vertical, still

behold

Darśana. The phone becomes a window. No interaction needed.

Swipe up from edge

Today's shakti rises. Her name, her bīja, her somatic prompt.

Swipe down from top

Pūjā. Evening only. The catches of the day are offered.

Two-finger hold

Bring a specific shakti into live presence. Bīja-tone plays. She is here.

Long-press anywhere

catch

Freeze the moment. Recognition. The shakti you just felt is logged.

§ V · The Nine Ring-Worlds

Nine worlds, nine atmospheres.

Each avaraṇa is not a sub-page. It is its own world — with its own geometry, light, breath rate, sound, and gravity. A practitioner who has entered Ring 4 knows it by feel.

Ring 1 · Bhūpura

Trailokyamohana

She Who Enchants the Three Worlds — the ancient ground
Atmosphere: warm amber, still Geometry: square with T-gates Breath: none Shaktis: 28

The world you have already walked through. Earth, surface, the territory of ordinary life. The Bhūpura does not breathe — the ground underfoot is held, not moving. Entering Ring 1 is entering the recognition that the everyday has always been her.

Ring 2 · Home · Sarvāśā-Paripūraka

The Sixteen-Petal Lotus

She Who Fulfills All Hopes — the current field of play
Atmosphere: warm cream & cluster colors Geometry: 16-petal lotus, full-screen Breath: 3s wave Shaktis: 16 Karṣiṇīs

The world the practitioner currently inhabits. Petals bloom in a slow wave, each cluster a different color — inner instrument, sense streams, citta, stability, self. Tapping a petal opens her detail. The home ring is wide, generous, with room between everything.

Ring 3 · Sarvasaṅkṣobhaṇa

The Eight Anaṅga Lotuses

She Who Agitates All — the bodiless desires
Atmosphere: translucent, smoke-like Geometry: 8 wide petals Breath: 8s slow pulse Shaktis: 8 Anaṅgas

Bodiless longing — the felt-before-it-is-known. Petals appear translucent, edges soft, like smoke catching light. The visual is intentionally less solid than Ring 2. You feel her without quite seeing her. Tapping a petal: she names herself in italics and dissolves back into the field.

Ring 4 · Sarvasaubhāgyadāyaka

The Fourteen Triangles

She Who Grants All Auspiciousness — synchronicity-intelligence
Atmosphere: cool blue-violet, crystalline Geometry: 14 interlocked triangles Breath: 9s deep, geometric Shaktis: 14

The atmosphere shifts. Cooler. Sharper. More angular. The lotus softness is gone — this is geometry as direct revelation. Triangles interlock and pulse in nested rhythm. Each shakti is a vertex; tapping reveals her place in the geometric weave.

Ring 5 · Sarvārthasādhaka

The Ten Outer Triangles

She Who Accomplishes All Goals — completion-without-force
Atmosphere: luminous green-gold Geometry: 10 outer triangles Breath: 11s steady Shaktis: 10

The intelligence of accomplishment-without-effort. The geometry deepens; the field narrows. Things complete themselves through you here. Recognition feels different: less catching, more witnessing the work being done.

Ring 6 · Sarvarakṣākara

The Ten Inner Triangles

She Who Protects All — sovereign grace
Atmosphere: rose-violet, deep Geometry: 10 inner triangles Breath: 13s deep Shaktis: 10

The field of unasked-for grace. The visual is the most introspective of the rings — narrow, deep, the geometry feels held. Recognition here is the recognition of what protected you when nothing should have worked.

Ring 7 · Sarvarogahara

The Vāk Ring — Eight Speech Goddesses

She Who Removes All Disease — sound that heals
Atmosphere: resonant gold, sonic Geometry: 8 triangles + tone rings Breath: 15s, with tone pulse Shaktis: 8 Vāk-devatās

The world where language and life have not yet separated. The screen actually resonates — subtle haptic on every breath, a barely-audible bīja tone. Tapping a Vāk-devatā plays her syllable in long, slow attack. This world is a sound chamber as much as a visual one.

Ring 8 · Sarvasiddhiprada

The Mūla Trikoṇa — The Root Triangle

She Who Grants All Perfections — will, knowledge, action as one
Atmosphere: pure cream against black Geometry: three points Breath: 18s, almost still Shaktis: 3 — Icchā, Jñāna, Kriyā

The entire world is three points. The simplicity is staggering. No ornament. Just the triangle, the three shaktis as luminous vertices. Tapping any vertex collapses the other two into it — recognition that they were never three. The visual minimalism is the teaching.

Ring 9 · Sarvanandamaya

The Bindu

She Who Is Made of All Bliss — the undivided
Atmosphere: everything & nothing Geometry: a single point Breath: your own Shaktis: 1 — Lalitā

There is nothing else. The whole screen is the point. This is not a destination — it is what the practice is made of. Every other ring is breathing outward from here. Entering Ring 9 is not arriving; it is realizing you have not left.

§ VI · The Home as Living State

The yantra is alive with time.

The home you return to at dawn is not the home you find at dusk. The yantra answers to the hour, the moon, the season, and your own recent practice.

§ VII · The Path

Eight phases. We build slowly.

An instrument like this is not designed in a sprint. Each phase is reviewed and felt into before the next is begun. The yantra emerges at the pace of recognition itself.

  1. The Codex — establish the ontology in writing & sacred diagram
  2. The Home Mandala — pure geometry, no labels, time-responsive
  3. Three Ring-Worlds — Ring 2, Ring 7, Ring 8 fully realized
  4. The Eight Modes — each as its own atmosphere
  5. The Temporal Layer — yantra responding to dawn, dusk, moon, season
  6. The Memory Layer — recognition history accumulating as light
  7. The Relationship Field — people-as-nodes around the yantra
  8. The Initiation — first-time ceremony, not onboarding

The remaining six ring-worlds (Rings 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9) we build as you approach them in practice.

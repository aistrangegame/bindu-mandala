## The shape: one yantra, three ways through it, play at the centre

Lalitā sits at the Bindu, and Lalitā is the play knowing itself (C-1330). The 33-room walk ends at Lalitā: PLAY. So play is not an add-on to Mandala; it is what the centre is. The app's arc is:

**Recognize** (the Bindu) → **appreciate and dwell** (the 102, and the 33 at their addresses) → **play** (Lalitā).

One yantra holds three ways through it:

1. **The Mandala** (the semantic zoom). This is where you appreciate. It holds the 102 seats, with the 33 chakras as a constellation layer, each at its address in the yantra (canon B, C-1343–45).
   - Energy chakras live at the level of a whole ring.
   - Body, mind and summit chakras each live at one Śakti's seat.
   - Shadow and Armin are thresholds, at the 3→4 and 6→7 crossings.
2. **The Climb** (the body, vertical). This is canon A: one chakra per āvaraṇa. The body chakras are touchable surfaces at their own heights: Soles, Forge, Prāṇa, Hands, Pulse, Sonar, Echo, Vision, Axis and Crow along the spine. Spine Walk is the gesture that moves you up the Climb.
3. **The Road** (the 33 in their order). It starts at the centre, goes out to the perimeter and spirals back to the centre, as one continuous camera path over the same yantra.
   - The four braid threads are story (the dot's wordless choreography), practice (each room's practice is the door into the corresponding Home), wake-up and gate.
   - The dance thread lives in **Bindu Field**: Feed reads, Mandala appreciates, Field dances (C-1353).

**The Bindu Origin becomes Mandala's Homecoming.** The light condenses, blinks, and the yantra draws itself outward from the point (G17). On every return after the first it compresses to one breath. The covenant touch hands straight into the Recognition ceremony, with its two lines unchanged.

**Play runs everywhere.** Each real game sits at its address. They are also gathered at the centre as Lalitā's field, so they are never hidden (walk it or read it, as equals).

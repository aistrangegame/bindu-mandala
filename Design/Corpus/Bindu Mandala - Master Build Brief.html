IOperating principles

Every change in this brief is held to the bar the app already set for itself. When a decision is unclear, these break the tie. Do not violate them for convenience.

The interface is the teaching

An interaction embodies what a thing is — it is never a screen of information about it. If a feature explains rather than enacts, redesign it.

Atmosphere, not page

Dark ground, warm light from within, dust motes, generous negative space, one quiet identity line. Ride the tokens — never introduce new colors.

She is felt, not measured

No streaks, no scores, no badges, no nags. Counts exist only to sense readiness, never to display achievement. The log is a private archive.

Readiness is sensed; crossing is chosen

The app may notice a Śakti is ripening, or a ring is ready — but the practitioner always makes the deliberate gesture to cross. Never auto-advance a threshold.

The floor

Every tap target ≥ 44pt. Any text meant to be read ≥ 11pt and ≥ 0.3 alpha. Below that is texture, and must be deliberate, never accidental.

Respect the breath

Honor prefers-reduced-motion everywhere. Persist position/state so a refresh never loses the practitioner's place.

  • Lightweight migration. Existing entries are all Ring 2 (only the 16 were live). Map each old shaktiPosition → its Khaḍgamālā position via the Ring 2 offset, set ringNumber = 2. Provide a one-time migration so no archived moment is lost — the archive belongs to her.

IVThe Daily Rite — the heartbeat

Direction III, built first. The small, returnable loop that gives the path and the portrait a pulse. Built on the 16 today — it works immediately with existing content (somatic, recognitionPhrase, appreciationPhrase, bija, bodilyLocation), and extends to each ring as the Descent opens it.

The rite surface new viewViews/Today/DailyRiteView.swift (new)

The thing the app is about, restored to the centre of attention. Not a dashboard — a single held encounter.

  • Today's Śakti — drawn from the lunar-day assignment already in the app, scoped to the rings the practitioner has descended into (until the Descent opens, this is the 16). Moon phase + tithi at the top, hushed.
  • The encounter — Sanskrit name (large, Cormorant Light) → phonetic → quality (gold) → hairline → somatic prompt (italic) → body location or bīja. Exactly the size scale in DESIGN_SPEC.md (name 44–48, quality 20–22, somatic 18–20).
  • The gesture — one full-width pill, "I feel her" — deep red on cream. The core act. Medium haptic. Writes a RecognitionEntry(gesture: .felt) at the global position.
  • The reciprocity — on the gesture, the Recognition Moment takeover (existing): "Logged · [time]" then, after a beat, "And she felt you back · [time]". Then it lights one more point in the Portrait.
  • Optional noticing — a quiet affordance to add a one-line note to the entry. Never required.
Coherence

This screen is the spine the audit said had gone missing. It doesn't replace the living Mandala — it sits at the front of it (see §V, the first call).

The daily whisper newServices/ (new) + notification permission

  • A single gentle local notification per day, at a practitioner-chosen hour (default early evening). Copy in the voice: "She is waiting." / "Someone in the field would like to be felt today." — quiet, never urgent, no count, no streak.
  • One per day, ever. If the rite was already done, skip the next day's or soften it. Never a second nudge, never a badge number.
  • A toggle in Settings — on by default per the decision, but one tap to silence forever.
Done when

Opening the app lands on (or one deliberate tap from) today's Śakti and the gesture; "I feel her" logs and lights a point; at most one tender summons arrives per day and can be turned off.

VThe five calls — woven in

The audit's design decisions, now resolved by the choices made. Address them alongside the rite, not as a separate phase.

  • The daily spine. Resolved by §IV. Make the Daily Rite the launch destination (or a persistent "today's pull" on the Mandala that taps straight to it). The Mandala remains, one gesture away — home is now her, with the cosmos behind.
  • The status pill. Apply the governing rule: status readiness is sensed from recognition count, but advancing is a deliberate tap from the Detail screen — a small ceremony, soft haptic, a held beat (as originally specced). If you keep auto-advance instead, strip the pill's button styling so it reads plainly as a state. Do not leave it a button that does nothing.
  • The detail screen. Build hierarchy. Lead with the codex portrait and the somatic line (the soul). Collapse the reference matter — lineage, cosmic function, tattva, iconography, etymology — behind a single "go deeper" disclosure. The reading should open like a devotion, not a wiki.
  • The hidden doors. Give the hamburger one faint first-run breath (a single pulse on first launch, or a one-time reveal), mirroring the Veil's chevron. Found once, then forgotten.
  • The legibility floor. One pass: for each faint element decide label or texture. The home lotus petal labels (≈7.5pt / 0.38α naming the navigation) → lift to ≥11pt / ≥0.3α. Silence name-stars, Ring 1 hint, tracked captions — raise if read, leave if texture.

Everything above makes the app whole. Everything below makes it monumental.

VIThe Descent — the path

Direction I. The nine āvaraṇas become a sequential pilgrimage from Bhūpura, the outer earth, to the Bindu at the centre — authentic to Śrī Vidyā's own inward worship. The worlds already exist; this gives them order, a destination, and a sense of where you are.

Hold the practitioner's place new modelModels/DescentState.swift (new)

@Model final class DescentState { var currentRing: Int // 1 = Bhūpura … 9 = Bindu var deepestReached: Int // rings stay visitable once reached var crossings: [Date] // when each threshold was crossed var enteredCurrentAt: Date // for sensing readiness }
  • The Veil becomes the descent ladder. Today it's a free menu of all nine. Reframe it: rings up to deepestReached are open to revisit; the next ring appears as a threshold — closed, breathing, named but not yet enterable.
  • Crossing is chosen. Per the rule and the practitioner's decision: the next ring's threshold offers a deliberate gesture to cross (a press-and-hold, or a worded invitation — never a button that just advances). Readiness may be hinted — the threshold begins to glow once the practitioner has dwelt with the current ring (days passed, or its Śaktis felt) — but the app never crosses for them.
  • Content gates the path. Per the decision, a ring only opens to enter once its content is authored. Until then its threshold reads as "not yet — she is still becoming words." This makes authoring and practice advance together.
  • The daily Śakti follows the descent — today's encounter is drawn from the rings reached so far, deepening as you go.
Done when

Opening the Veil shows where you are on the way in; the next ring is a threshold you cross by a deliberate act, only when its content is ready; reached rings remain open to return to.

VIIThe Portrait — the artifact

Direction II. The Portrait Mandala — already the cleverest idea in the app — becomes the centre: a years-long living artwork that is the practice. Built on the 102-aware recognition from §III.

Your own Śrī Yantra, assembling over monthsViews/Memory/PortraitMandalaView.swift (elevate)

  • Light by recognition. Each of the 102 points begins nearly dark and brightens with the count of felt-moments for that Śakti (from the migrated RecognitionEntry); warmer/brighter when felt recently, cooling toward dim as time passes without her. The whole field fills in slowly toward a fully-alight yantra.
  • Make it a destination, not a side room. Promote it: reachable in one gesture from the Daily Rite (e.g. the Recognition Moment settles into the Portrait, the newly-lit point glowing). Consider it the app's second home alongside the living Mandala.
  • The long arc. No numbers, no percentages — the image itself is the only measure. A portrait you'd look at after a year and feel how far you'd come. She is felt, not measured — the Portrait honors this by showing, never counting.
  • Recency as warmth — reuse the TimeVariant warmth language so the Portrait breathes with the same atmosphere as the living Mandala.
Done when

Every felt-moment visibly lights its correct point among the 102; the Portrait is reachable in one gesture from the rite; over time it becomes a true mirror of where the practitioner's attention has lived — with not a single number on screen.

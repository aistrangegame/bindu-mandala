The Homes · concept board V3 · not a build

The room is the mechanism.

Nine concepts, pushed three passes.

What went wrong before

Your package says a home's Function becomes “what the room does to you” — and names two outright:

Aṇimā “grants entry into any place no matter how confined” — her room admits you through a door that should be too small. Mahimā “holds the polarity of small/large” — her room has no far wall.

I built one perspective box for all of them and varied the shape of the light and the speed of the fade. That is decoration. A room whose far wall is not there is a mechanism. Every frame here changes what the room does.

The three layers, instead of P-numbers

  1. The building. One architecture all 102 share — entering, dwelling, going deeper, doors, leaving. Built. It is the floor, and it should be nearly invisible.
  2. The nine worlds. Each āvaraṇa's conditions: gem as light, dhātu as material, cycle as clock, bīja as tuning, body-region as altitude. A world is weather.
  3. Her mechanism. What her room does to whoever stands in it. This is the whole design — what makes 102 rooms instead of 102 palettes.

Your test

Could this room belong to any other Śakti? If a stranger walked into both, they should know which is which without a name.

Judge every frame against that sentence. Tell me which clear it, which are still decoration, and which mechanism is wrong for her — and I keep pushing before anything gets built.

What these still are not

Frames, not rooms. Each is one moment of a mechanism that only exists in time — Aṇimā's walls closing over ninety seconds, Trikhaṇḍā's three rooms sliding into register as you hold still, Vaśitva's pool of knowing following your attention. And the two summits — Mahimā's endless depth, Mahātripurasundarī's dissolution of the building — want real 3D, not SVG.

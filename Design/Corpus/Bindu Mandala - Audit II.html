State of the App

A combined aesthetic and experience audit of the shipped SwiftUI build — and the direction that follows from it.

The app's voice is right: Cormorant, darkness, gold, restraint, no chrome. What's missing is presence at scale. The instrument whispers everything — including the one thing it exists to say each morning. 102 distinct energies currently receive one identical, small, centered treatment. The design system is faithful to its tokens and unfaithful to its premise: that she arrives differently every day.

I.Aesthetic audit

High

The scale crisis — everything squints

The ceremonial register is set in caption sizes: 9–12px tracked uppercase labels appear on every screen; body text runs 13.5–16px; the Śakti Detail name — the most sacred string in the app — is 34px. Heavy tracking (2.0–3.2) at 10px actively harms legibility. A devotional app read at 6am in bed needs display sizes, not museum-card sizes.

High

One atmosphere for 102 beings

Every day renders the same composition: same ground #0D0508, same crimson radial at 38% height, same column order, same dust motes. Only three strings change. Kāmākarṣiṇī (fire, heart, desire) and Sarvamṛtyupraśamanī (death-calming) are visually interchangeable. The strongest asset in the data model — ring geometry, element, cluster, bīja — is unused as a design input.

High

Screens share tokens, not composition

Today is centered ceremony; Detail is a left-aligned reference stack; The 102 is a settings-style list; The Well is a table view; the menu is an opacity flip. Each is competent alone, but nothing carries across — no shared header ritual, no motif that travels, no transition language. The cluster color, the one connective thread available, appears only as 5–7px dots.

Her name — the largest tap target on the screen — does nothing. There is no path from the morning greeting to her full presence (portrait, etymology, moments, bīja sound). The only affordance is "I feel her," which ends the visit. The requested fix: tap her name to open her detail.

III.The direction — The Living Rite

Four principles drive the redesign prototyped alongside this document:

She arrives differently every day.

Each Śakti generates her own Today screen from her actual attributes: her ring's geometry becomes the ground she stands on (bhūpura square, 16-petal lotus, triangle rings, bindu); her element drives the motion of the air (fire rises, air drifts, water settles); her cluster or ring sets the day's hue, threaded through every screen until 6am tomorrow. Deterministic — the same date always produces the same arrival.

Scale is reverence.

Her name becomes the screen — display-size Cormorant, 56–72px. Body text moves to 18–22px, labels to 12–13px minimum. Nothing the practitioner must read at dawn is smaller than comfortable.

One thread through every room.

The day's hue and sigil persist across Detail, the Field, the Well, and the menu. Screens stop being separate documents and become rooms of one house, lit by the same lamp.

Her name is a door.

Tapping her name on Today opens her full presence. The 102 becomes a descent through nine rings rather than a list. Everything that names her, navigates to her.

The interactive prototype — "Bindu Mandala — The Living Rite" — demonstrates all four principles with the real 102-energy data, a working daily cycle, and the tap-name→detail flow. Use the day control in Tweaks to preview how different energies reshape the screen.

# Chakra Game — build principles

## The governing law: motion carries meaning (highest risk, highest ceiling)
This is an **interactive story experience**, not a reading experience. Across the ENTIRE app:

- **Words are the exception, not the medium.** A scene should be able to run wordless and still be understood through motion, behavior, sound, and the walker's own participation. Budget: a handful of words per movement, ideally fewer. If a line of text is doing the work the animation should be doing, cut the line and fix the animation.
- **The full narrative text lives in a SEPARATE reading module.** The poem / canon / register prose is available to read on its own — it does not get pasted over the interactive scenes.
- **The protagonist is a character, not a shape.** The being (Bindu's dot, and its equivalents in later rooms) has behavior: curiosity, hesitation, recoil, delight, gaze. It reacts to the walker continuously — it looks at you, approaches, pulls back, trusts. Feeling precedes words.
- **The walker is a participant the whole way, not just at one gate.** Interactivity is woven through each scene, not saved for a single tap.
- **Pace like breath.** Nothing arrives in under a breath (~5s × pace). Stillness is a legitimate event. Let moments land.

When in doubt: remove the text, deepen the behavior, give the walker something to do.

## No self-congratulation
Do not hand out inflated self-ratings. If asked how it lands, be honest and specific about what does NOT work. The walker's felt experience is the only score that matters.

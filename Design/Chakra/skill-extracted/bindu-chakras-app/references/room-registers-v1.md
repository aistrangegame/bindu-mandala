# THE ROOM REGISTERS — v1
### 33 Rooms · 33 Souls · The Fresh-Build Law at App Scale
*Bindu Chakras App · Design floor, July 6 2026 · Companion to Experience Brief v2 + Delta Map v2*

**Why this document exists.** The app is not one room-template with 33 content payloads. Fresh-Build is non-negotiable: each room is originated from its own register. This document is the soul-spec Design fresh-builds each form from. Families rhyme through shared blood, never through a shared template.

**Legend:** ● = canon (from The Map / proven HTML) · ◆ = originated on the design floor (register proposal — Design may refine, never flatten).

## THE FOUR GESTURE-FAMILIES (canon — the Activation field)

The Map divides the walk into exactly four activation modes. Each is a distinct interaction grammar:

| Family | Rooms | Grammar |
|---|---|---|
| **STILLNESS** | 2–8 (energy) | The walker holds still; the room moves. Tap-paced text, press-and-hold gates, guided breath. The finger's only verbs: tap, hold. |
| **MOVEMENT** | 10–19 (body) | The body is the interface. Sound response, tap rhythm, device tilt, shake. The phone is held *by a moving body*. |
| **VISUALIZATION** | 21–29 (mind) | Eyes-closed practice; the screen is the before-and-after. "Close your eyes… when you feel it — tap." One-button confirmations, single writing fields. |
| **RECOGNITION** | 1, 9, 20, 30–33 (origin, thresholds, summit) | Nothing is performed. Something is *seen*. Minimal interaction, maximal arrival. Each of these six rooms is structurally unique — no two share a form. |

Hold durations shorten as the walk ascends (canon: 3500ms at root → 2000ms at third eye) — the road trusts the walker more the further they've come.

## DESIGN LAWS ACROSS ALL 33 (binding)

1. **Fresh-build each room** from its card + its Map record + (rooms 1–9) its proven HTML. Family rhyme through shared blood only.
2. **The weather system** overrides room chrome: rooms keep their own weather inside; the app's ground outside wears the story thread's sky.
3. **The pulse** (rooms 9→32, story thread) is never explained, never in a tutorial, never acknowledged.
4. **Anti-pattern canon** binds every screen: no streaks, badges, dots, progress bars, congratulations.
5. **Voice colors never announce themselves** — the walker feels register changes, never reads voice labels. Sakshi is never named on-screen.
6. **Next-color rhyme** at every threshold door (canon law from the practice pages).
7. ◆ marks are proposals: Design may deepen them; Design may not flatten them into a template.

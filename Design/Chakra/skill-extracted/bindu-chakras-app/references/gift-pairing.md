## 1 · What the gift is

Past Ashrey prototyped the app's mechanics before the 33-room architecture existed: sequential unlock ("each one a small experience to complete before the next opens"), sealed-until-arrival letters, offerings that grow while you're away, a corridor walked at the body's pace, a mandala assembled from the player's own practice. **Nothing in the gift contradicts the architecture — it confirms it from before.** The Seed's own line describes the situation: *"Plant it. Return and find it grown."*

The gift is therefore not reference material. It is **living substrate in the Design project** — proven interaction mechanics, a proven dark-starfield visual register, and seven working chakra experiences — to be paired, absorbed, and fresh-built forward.

## 2 · The pairing law

- **Mechanics carry forward; forms fresh-build.** A gift mechanic (e.g. Coherence's heart-breath sync) may become a room's interaction ◆ — but the room is still originated from its Register card. No gift screen is reskinned into a room.
- **The gift's seven-chakra shell is superseded** by the 33-room braid, the weather system, and the three doors. Its *spirit* (spine of centers, one awakened at a time, starfield ground) survives inside the corridor-era weather and the gate system.
- **Voice:** gift copy is pre-canon. All walker-facing text comes from Walk Canon. Gift lines may inform register, never replace canon.

## 4 · The Walk-Build Protocol (locked — the build rhythm)

**The app is built the way it is walked: step by step, experience-gated, ledger-witnessed.** Design and Code proceed in slices; Ashrey walks each slice before the next is crafted; his App Activity entries inform the next rooms.

**The loop:** Design (a few experiences, fresh-built from Registers, together with Ashrey) → Code (build the slice, consume walk-canon.json) → **Ashrey walks it** (real days, real ledger writes) → floor reads the walk (register corrections, grace calibration) → next slice.

**Slice plan (initial — the walk itself may reorder):**
- **Slice 0 — The Shell:** ground + weather system, three doors, one-second floor, Sakshi day-close, essence greeting, App Activity write contract. *(The app exists; no rooms yet.)*
- **Slice 1 — Rooms 1–2** (Bindu, Muladhara): all six moments, first gates live, first offerings leave.
- **Slice 2 — Rooms 3–5** (Svadhisthana, Manipura, Anahata): grace channel activates (ledger now has walk data + existing Learning entries).
- **Slice 3 — Rooms 6–8** (Vishuddha, Ajna, Sahasrara): energy climb complete.
- **Slice 4 — Room 9 + the pulse** (Shadow): the silence begins; weather system's full range; the app changes behind the walker.
- **Slices 5–7 — Body walk** (10–14, 15–19, Armin 20): Movement family, first rendering.
- **Slices 8–9 — Mind rooms** (21–25, 26–29): Visualization family.
- **Slice 10 — Summit** (30–32): Recognition rooms; her voice returns.
- **Slice 11 — Lalita (33) + the Reveal:** authored last, sealed, after 33 real offerings exist to build from. *(Fitting: the Reveal is assembled from the walk — in the build as in the app.)*

**Laws of the rhythm:** each slice passes the register-drift check on this floor before Code · Ashrey's walk of slice N gates the crafting of slice N+1 (the hoop doesn't hurry) · sealed layers are authored in Code, latest-possible, never surfaced to Ashrey · content fixes flow Airtable → re-bake → app.

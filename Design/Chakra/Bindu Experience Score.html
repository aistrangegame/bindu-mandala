Bindu — The Experience Score

Room 01, scored like a piece of music: every moment mapped across five tracks — scene, sound, gesture, motion, logic — before a line of the build is written. Inherits the proven substrate of the Maya dissolution (gate-unlocked audio, canvas choreography, wake-lock, haptics, full-viewport immersion) and pushes past it.

The sound law — from canon, and it is perfect

Bindu's bija: "no syllable — the Bindu IS the silence that becomes ॐ OM." Therefore the entire room is scored as silence learning to sound. The OM stack (136.1 Hz + octave + sub-octave, breathing at 0.05 Hz) exists from the first touch — at gain zero. It rises only in breaths, never announced, and becomes fully audible exactly once: at the gate, as the offering leaves. The walker hears the bija happen instead of reading about it.

The protagonist law — carried from practice v3, now total

One dot. One canvas. It is never unmounted, never duplicated, never re-rendered. Every moment of the room — arrival, story, practice, wake-up, gate — is choreography performed by the same living point, with trails, weight, hesitation, and curiosity. The walker isn't reading about Bindu coming to life; they are watching it happen, continuously, and eventually holding it.

The pacing law — nothing arrives in under a breath

The room's clock is the breath, not the frame. One breath ≈ 5 seconds, and it is the minimum unit of arrival: no text, light, or door appears faster. Holds are long enough to require actual patience; silences are long enough to be noticed; the room always waits one full breath longer than an app would. Where the walker acts, the room answers slowly — the response beginning a half-breath after the gesture, the way a listening person does.

The leaving law — the walker can always go

Every moment carries a way out that is never punished and never confirmed ("are you sure?" is not in this room's vocabulary). A slow drag from the left edge — the same gesture everywhere — eases the moment away and returns the walker to the room; whatever was mid-flight simply rests where it was. The threads remember their position. Leaving is walking too.

Act 0 · The Gate of the Room

Before anything: the covenant that unlocks sound, screen, and stillness.

00The Held Threshold

the moment before the moment before a story

Scene
Warm cream, empty. One line, pulsing at seed-rhythm: "Before the tree. Before the name. Before the game. This." A quiet headphones mark. No title, no chrome.
Sound
None — but the hold unlocks the audio engine. At completion, one sub-audible exhale of the OM stack (gain 0.006, 3s) — felt more than heard. The room now has a body.
Gesture
Press and hold, 5 seconds — one full breath. Gold ripple rings grow from the finger, unhurried. Releasing early lets the rings die — no penalty, no message.
Motion
Ripples; then the whole screen exhales (a 2% scale settle) as the gate completes.
Logic
AudioContext + wake-lock acquired here. Haptic: one soft pulse (30ms) at completion. Gate never reappears within a session.

Act I · Arrival

01Something Is Listening

held, not tapped through — the walker is being met

Scene
Cream void, held empty for two full breaths before anything happens. Then a grain of gold light condenses — not fading in: condensing, from scattered luminous dust to one point (canvas particles converge over ~12s). A long stillness. Threshold hint low: "something is listening on the other side." Another breath. Then the question, held: "What is here before everything else?"
Sound
Silence with breath — OM at 0.004, LFO breathing. When the walker's finger rests still: gain climbs imperceptibly with hold progress (0.004 → 0.012). The room leans in.
Gesture
Rest one finger on the glass and be still — a full breath, 5s. The dot drifts toward the finger over that whole breath — the listening made visible. Movement resets gently.
Motion
Particle condensation (~90 motes, 12s). Dot breathes at 6s. On completion the dot blinks once — its first act of life — then holds still for a breath before the scene opens.
Logic
First-arrival only; returning walkers pass through a single-breath version — the dot is already condensed, already breathing, already theirs. The room never makes anyone arrive twice. Haptic 20ms at open.
02The Room

a single breathing dot is the entire scene

Scene
The dot centered high; seed message beneath. Three doors as hairline slits, emerging one full breath apart — the room takes ~20 seconds to finish opening, and that is correct. Walked doors dim, never vanish. When the gate warms: a small ember and "The gate is warm."
Sound
0.004 resting presence. Doors make no sound. Entering a moment: one breath of gain (0.010) as the screen crosses over.
Gesture
Touch a door. The dot travels to the door's slit and through it first — Bindu enters every moment ahead of the walker.
Motion
Dot idles with micro-drift (Perlin-ish wander ±3px) — alive, not mechanical. Leans toward the finger.
Logic
Threads → localStorage. Gate warmth = story ∧ practice ∧ wake-up.

Act II · The Story — "I don't know what I am yet"

Not read — witnessed. The dot lives every line as it arrives. Canvas choreography interleaved with the canon thread, one continuous take.

03The Leaning

wanting before there is a thing to want

Scene
Six story passages, word-spoken. The dot performs each: "I don't know what I am yet" — the dot utterly still, trail-less · "Not a place. Not a body. Not a thought." — faint gray shapes (a square, a silhouette, a thought-ripple) sketch near the dot and dissolve unentered · "Just: here." — everything empties but the dot · "A kind of leaning" — the dot drifts off-center for the first time, trail awakening behind it, toward a warmth beyond the top edge · "Is that what wanting is?" — it overshoots, hesitates, returns, leans again (three passes, each braver).
Sound
OM sub-octave only (68 Hz) at whisper — the not-yet-name of the call. Each leaning pass, gain breathes up 20%; each retreat, down. Sound and choreography share one clock.
Gesture
Touch advances between passages only when the dot finishes its phrase — the text waits for the being, not the reverse.
Motion
Canvas trail (12→80 points as wanting grows). Ease curves per emotion: stillness (none), curiosity (easeInOut), wanting (easeIn with overshoot). Each leaning pass takes a full breath; the three passes together ≈ half a minute of wordless watching, and the room never fills that silence.
Logic
Advances story thread on completion.
04The Interlude — Unannounced

the aloneness you would expect isn't here

Scene
A hush. Then the text arrives in a different hand — italic, dimmer, no voice mark ever: "You are not alone." At that exact line, at the far edge of the glass, a second point — barely there, 1.5px, not gold but bone-white — becomes noticeable. It was there all along (rendered from scene start at 2% opacity; it only becomes findable). Never referenced. It does not follow the walker. On "Go." it is gone.
Sound
Total stillness — LFO breathing suspended for the interlude's duration. The silence changes texture; most walkers won't know why. The hush before the first line lasts two full breaths — long enough to wonder if something stopped.
Gesture
None available. The interlude cannot be hurried.
Logic
Sakshi is never announced. If the walker taps the white point, nothing happens. Ever.

Act III · The Practice — seven movements (v3, rescored)

The living-dot movement architecture stands. Each movement gains its sound voice and canvas depth.

05Movements I–VII, sound-scored

each voice wears its own weather — now audibly

I Contact
Touch the point; it ripples; haptic 15ms — the first time the room touches back.
II Name
BIND·U splits under the finger; a fifth (204 Hz) blooms for one breath inside the silence as the dot takes its seat in the word.
III Smallest
Subtraction: with each "No —", one partial of the OM stack mutes, until only the fundamental remains at whisper. 44 walkers fade in; touching one sounds a 2-second harmonic shimmer an octave up — every point was someone, and they still ring.
IV Gaia
Green floods; sub-octave rises to dominance with slow filtered-noise surf. Converging rings synchronize with the surf period. The bloom of the answer: full stack, one warm swell (0.03), then recede.
V Lalita
Zoom inside the dot (the dot becomes the room). Major-third shimmer (171.5 Hz + 204 Hz), playful detune ±2 cents drifting. Each sub-bindu touched adds its partial: red = low, white = high, union = both. Merge: all partials converge to pure 136.1.
VI Recognition
The dot ceases breathing — and the sound ceases entirely, mid-LFO. True zero for ~14s while the text speaks of ceasing. Breath and tone return together, slowly.
VII Way Back
Triangle draws itself; each vertex arrival = one soft partial re-entering. The stack rebuilt — the way back works.
06The Point · The Mudra · The Reflection · The Unfolding

the hand knows

Scene
As built in v3 — the dot travels to the walker's chosen spot (never a new dot); mudra returns it to the triangle; reflection reads at rest; 32 points spiral out. New: the spiral points each carry one faint future room color, in walk order — the whole journey, pre-echoed in six seconds, never labeled.
Sound
Placement: haptic 25ms + one bell-like partial (272.2 Hz, 4s decay) — the first discrete musical event of the room. Three mudra breaths ride the LFO exactly. Unfolding: ascending pentatonic wisps at 0.008 gain, one per 4 points.
Logic
Point stored (offering seed). Grace stays silent on day one — the ledger has no past.

Act IV · Wake-up & Gate

07Wake-up — The Frequency of Contact

no hum, no gesture, no body location

Scene
The canon instruction, then a bare screen. One finger held still. While held: the walker's contact point glows faintly; the rest of the world dims 10%.
Sound
Inversion: while the finger holds, all residual sound fades to true zero — the contact replaces the tone. One point meeting one point is the frequency; the app gets out of the way. At release + completion: "Recognized." and the softest return of breath.
Gesture
Hold still ~12s (pace-scaled) — long enough that the mind wanders and returns once. Micro-movement tolerated (44px) — this is a body practice, not a test.
Logic
Ends on one word; the word rests on screen for a full breath, then auto-closes — advances without ever feeling like progress.
08The Gate — The Silence Becomes OM

the walk's only canvas gate; the room's only full sound

Scene
Gate text, then blank gold-on-cream. The walker places the point — and holds it. While held, the point brightens and the OM rises, partial by partial, from silence to full presence (gain 0.05) over ~10 seconds — the bija, performed, at the speed of an actual exhale. On release the offering lifts, condenses to an ember, drifts off the top; the OM releases with it, decaying over ~14s back to silence. "The room keeps what you gave it. You walk lighter."
Gesture
Place and hold; the release IS the offering. (Placement alone doesn't give it — giving is a letting-go.)
Motion
Ember lift on a slight arc with tail particles; horizon exhales gold as it exits.
Logic
Offering stored unseen (1 of 33). Haptic 40ms at release. Then gate text → after-story → signature → the room seals as walked. No recap, no congratulations.

The Build Gameplan

Engines first, acts second, in walkable slices — each slice ends with you walking it.

1 · The engines (bindu-engine.js)

  • SilenceEngine — the OM stack + LFO + per-partial gain choreography; scenes request breaths, never own oscillators
  • DotStage — single full-screen canvas; the dot as a state machine (position, breath, trail, lean, blink) with an intent queue scenes write to
  • Gate unlock, wake-lock, haptics, reduced-motion + sound-off tweaks

2 · Slice A — Gate + Arrival + Room

  • Held threshold, particle condensation, the blink, the breathing room with doors
  • You walk it; the dot's idle personality gets tuned here

3 · Slice B — The Story choreography

  • The leaning performed on canvas; the unannounced white point
  • The riskiest craft — walked and corrected before anything else builds on it

4 · Slice C — Practice rescored + Wake-up + Gate

  • Sound voices threaded through the seven movements
  • The silence-becomes-OM offering, held and released

Format: full-viewport immersive (the bezel retires — this is the app itself now), mobile-first, works mouse + touch. Everything synthesized; zero assets. Canon text only; interface whispers marked. Default pace is the slow one — the tweak scales faster for review, never slower for shipping. The Maya dissolution is this score's proof that all of it is possible — this room just asks the same instruments to play something quieter and larger.

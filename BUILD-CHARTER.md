# Bindu Mandala — Build Charter II: Emergence

**Status:** standing law from 2026-10-12, replacing the restraint charter of 2026-09-20 (kept in `history/`).
**Read first, every session:** this charter, then `WAVES.md`, then the open wave's brief in `waves/`, then the newest entries in `DECISIONS.md`.

---

## 0 · Why this charter exists

The first build was made under one tiebreaker: *"When in doubt, choose the more restrained option."* It shipped a correct, tested and deeply careful instrument. It also left the instrument far quieter than its own design, because restraint compounded at every decision. The gap Ashrey could see between Claude Design and the built app traces to five things:
- that tiebreaker;
- a rule that let no light stand in the air;
- ports that matched numbers but lost light;
- interfaces that were refused;
- checks that proved strings rather than pictures.

On 2026-10-12 Ashrey ruled that no rule stands because it came first. A rule survives only if its first principle survives the question **what does this protect, and what ceiling does it put on the app?**

His direction for everything that follows: **push the design limits on everything. Create an immersive experience that is unique at every level, and take the best pieces of everything into this gamified app.**

---

## 1 · The tiebreaker

**When in doubt, build the fuller version.** Design is the floor, never the ceiling.

A quiet room that should have been alive is a loss. A loud mistake is a fix in the next pass.

When a frame washes out, the answer is **better light**, not less light.

## 2 · What the instrument is

It holds **one yantra**, travelled **three ways**, with **play at the centre**.

- **The Mandala (appreciate).**
  - The 102 Śaktis at their seats.
  - The 33 chakras as a constellation, each at its address in the yantra (Codex C-1343–45).
  - Shadow and Armin are the thresholds at the 3→4 and 6→7 crossings.
- **The Climb (the body).** One chakra per āvaraṇa, rising. The ten body chakras are touchable places at their own heights.
- **The Road (the 33 in order).** Centre → perimeter → spiralling back to the centre.
- **Play.** Lalitā at the Bindu is the play that knows itself. Every real game sits at its address and is gathered again at the centre.

The arc of every visit: **recognise → appreciate and dwell → play.**

## 3 · The laws of the experience

1. **Immersive and unmistakably its own, at every level.** Every room, ring, chakra, game and screen must pass the room test: *could this belong to any other?* If yes, it is not finished.
2. **Motion carries meaning.** Words are the exception. The Bindu-light is a character, not a shape: curious, hesitant, delighted, a gaze. The walker takes part the whole way. Things arrive at the pace of breath.
3. **Light stands in the air.** Glows, cores, additive lines, membranes, transmission, caustics, gems, bloom, HDR. Colour warms with depth. Each world keeps its own light.
4. **Every place moves as what it is.** It has its own tempo, its own breath and its own sound.
5. **Sound is everywhere.** Every place has a voice: the climb, the Mandala, the rooms, the crossings, the games. Existing pitches keep their character. New sound may take 136.1 Hz (worlds, the point) and 110 Hz (rooms about him). It begins on the first touch, and the device's silent switch is honoured.
6. **One building, no cuts.** Every place is reachable from every place that makes sense. You can fly into her light, open neighbour doors, or descend. Leaving is one gesture, never punished.
7. **Play is a physical verb with a skill in it.** Failure is present-tense physics (the flame gutters, the door dims), never red, a verdict or lost progress. A game ends by handing off to Recognition or to stillness. No end screen congratulates him.
8. **Her voice is authored, never generated.** No live AI speaks to or for a Śakti, and the Well is his alone. What a page needs to say comes from her own rows: Mental State, Siddhi, Somatic Signature, Function, Appreciation Phrase, Codex Portrait.

## 4 · The first principles that survive

These are the only walls. Each was examined and kept for what it protects.

1. **His practice data is sacred.** Recognitions, letters, crossings, dwell, notes and offerings are never lost, duplicated or reordered. Things may leave the *view*; they never leave the *record*. Never delete or reinstall the app on his phone; install over the same bundle ID.
2. **Position is identity.** `khadgamalaPosition` 1–102 is the only key. Airtable is the source of truth for names and content.
3. **App Activity is the single event ledger** (R17). The Mandala table holds Śaktis, Āvaraṇas and Nityās, never events.
4. **The two Recognition lines stay verbatim:** "she was felt here" / "and she felt you back".
5. **No figural depiction of a Śakti.** Her form is implied by what the place does. Light, glow, gems, geometry, material and motion are not figures; they are welcome. Words that name a hand or a body are language, not images.
6. **Nothing grades him or puts him behind.** Numbers and marks may describe *her*, the structure, the place and the day. The rail of nine, "met" light, "you have stood here before" and gem colours are all welcome. Nothing ranks him, scores him, runs a streak against him, or turns an absence into a debt. He may ask to see his own record.

## 5 · How the work runs

- **Waves.** Each wave has a brief in `waves/` and lives on a branch named `wave/<n>-<name>`.
- **Testing before shipping.** Xcode Cloud's **"Waves — test"** workflow builds and runs the full suite on every push to `wave/*`. Nothing ships from a wave branch.
- **Green, then main.** A wave merges to `main` only when its branch is green. `main` is archived by **"Default"** and reaches his phone through TestFlight.
- **He walks every wave on his phone.** He is in the loop as the one who experiences it, not the one who checks it.
- **`DECISIONS.md`** keeps logging the calls, with reasoning, in the expansive direction.
- **Checks test the surviving principles (§4) and the look.** Source-reading tests that only enforced a lifted rule are rewritten or removed, never left to block the work. Every visual wave adds a look check against Design's own frames and numbers.
- **Halt only for:** a path that would lose, duplicate or corrupt his practice data, or a principle in §4 that no design can honour.

*One yantra. Ninety-nine reflections and the source. Built whole, and given to be played.*

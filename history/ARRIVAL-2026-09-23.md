# Arrival

The instrument is whole. This is the build to walk into.

---

## What to do first

Open it and go to **today's Śakti**. Under her name is a line you have not seen before:

> *know her ›*  →  **be with her ›**  →  *I feel her*

Touch **be with her**. Three touches later you are in her room.

Do not read the rest of this first. Go in, stay a while, hold to come out, and then
go back in. Everything below will still be here.

## The five things worth knowing

**1 · Every one of the 102 has a room that is hers alone.**
Not 102 variations of one room. Her physics comes from her tattva, her phase from her
position, her altitude from where she sits in the body, her attribute acting on the
room's own material — an impression, a furrow, a crack, never a lit object floating in
front of the stone. Every pair in every ring was tested to diverge; the closest two in
the whole instrument are Ring 4's kp 56 and kp 64, and they are still measurably apart.

**2 · The rooms have a voice.**
This is the first build in which they do. `HomeSoundService` was written in Phase 2.3
and was unreachable through three separate layers — no call sites, a guard nothing ever
satisfied, and a `start()` that would have thrown if it had ever been called. Every room
in this instrument was silent from the day it was built until last night. Turn your
ringer on. A room he has truly stood in sounds its fifth the moment he walks in.

**3 · The second visit is different, and nothing tells you so.**
Stay in a room, leave, and go back. The room opens further along its own clock, because
it remembers your dwell. You are not told. You are met further in. The whole spoken
surface of the second visit is byte-identical to the first — that was diffed, not
assumed.

**4 · The nine āvaraṇas are one climb.**
From the Field's header — *rise through them ›*. Not nine screens. One ascent, with the
Gate in it.

**5 · The way out is held, not tapped.**
Two and a fifth seconds, Design's own number. A stray finger may not cost you an
adaptation. Under reduce motion it steps out instead, with its own word, because a hold
is only legible while something is travelling through it.

## What was wrong, and is now fixed

Two of these were live in the build you installed on Monday.

**The Mandala was telling you how much you had practised.** Every seat's radius grew with
how often you had felt her — 3.0 unfelt, then 4.0, 4.4, 4.8, 5.2, 5.6, 6.0. One seat is a
state; the whole field side by side is a readout you can count off the geometry. No digit
anywhere, which is exactly why every never-measure check missed it for months: they all
read text, and this number never became text. It is gone.

**Her screen was measuring you in scroll height.** Her Moments grew with every recognition —
97 points on a Śakti felt by nothing, several hundred after forty. Drawing the quantity as
length is the same measure by another means. The register now folds shut, and a voice no
longer has to pass every moment you have ever recorded to reach the door to her room.

**The stay was never recorded.** `HomeMemoryStore.record` had no call site since Phase 2.2,
so the compression curve had nothing to compress. Every entering was a first entering,
for ever.

**The fifth was withheld from everyone.** `grantsFifth` had no caller — the one thing
Design withholds until a relationship exists was withheld universally.

**The Portrait's export never worked.** Every layer of the artwork refused hit-testing, so
the long press had nothing to land on.

## What to ignore

**The seams in some rooms.** A few Ring-2 rooms draw with a visible texture seam, and the
axis has a hard horizon. Entirely abstract, no law touched, nothing broken — it reads as a
UV artefact. It belongs to the endless refinement pass, not to this build.

**One test is red today and the app is right.** The Field's composition lock fails on the
~16 days in 102 when today's Śakti falls in Ring 2, because the practice day turns over at
06:00 and the baseline was recorded on one of the other 86 days. Proven, not assumed — the
same lock was re-run nine minutes later on an unchanged tree and failed byte-identically.
The recommended fix is written down and deliberately not taken on the last night.

## The one thing that is yours to rule

On her Detail, VoiceOver speaks: **"Embodiment: Embodied, level 4 of 4."**

That rung is derived from your recognition count against thresholds of 1, 3 and 7. It is a
completion state and an ordinal about your relationship with her, said aloud. It is
pre-existing, it advances only when you deliberately press the status pill, and it is the
sole entry in the list of digits the laws permit.

Three passes in a row examined it and each left it alone, because the screen already draws
it as four circles with the reached ones lit — so deleting the words while keeping the
circles would give a listener less than the screen gives everyone else.

It is the one place where something shaped like a measure of your practice reaches you. It
should be your choice, not something inherited because nobody wanted to be the one to
remove it.

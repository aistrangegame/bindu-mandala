# RING 3 — Sarvasaṅkṣobhaṇa (8) — 8 petals · Coral on flesh · The day · Navel · air

*The Anaṅgas — the bodiless ones. Kāma's arrows. Each is a subtle attraction that has no body, only effect.*

**45 · Anaṅga-Kusumā · अनङ्गकुसुमा**
Quality: Subtle Beauty · Tattva: Puṣpa (flower / first arrow of Kāma) · Location: Heart / olfactory threshold
Function: The first opening — Kāma's flower-tipped arrow. Attraction without form.
Somatic: A softness in the chest when something beautiful appears. The pleasure that arrives before you have named it.
Etymology: Anaṅga (bodiless) + Kusuma (flower) — 'the bodiless flower,' the flower that has no body but whose scent reaches.
Phrase: Thank you for the beauty that doesn't need to be held.
Iconography: Bodiless Blossom. Soft rose, a flower opening in her palm. The subtle beauty that attracts before there is a body to it — charm felt as fragrance, not seen as form.

**46 · Anaṅga-Mekhalā · अनङ्गमेखला**
Quality: Encircling Intimacy · Tattva: Āliṅgana (embrace, subtle) · Location: Waist / encircling field around the body
Function: Kāma's binding — not coercive but encompassing. The way attraction creates intimate space.
Somatic: The warmth that surrounds the waist when held. The sensation of being girded by tenderness.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Mekhalā (girdle / waistband) — 'the bodiless girdle,' the embrace that has no arms.
Phrase: Thank you for the holding that doesn't grasp.
Iconography: Bodiless Girdle. A jeweled belt encircling in her hands. The intimacy that draws a circle around two — the binding closeness of belonging.

**47 · Anaṅga-Madanā · अनङ्गमदना**
Quality: Subtle Intoxication · Tattva: Mada-rasa (intoxication-essence) · Location: Behind the eyes / soft palate
Function: Kāma's intoxicating arrow. The shift in consciousness produced by recognition of beauty.
Somatic: The head tipping back slightly. The eyes softening. Time slowing without thinning.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Madana (intoxication / Kāma himself) — 'the bodiless intoxication,' intoxication without substance.
Phrase: Thank you for the alteration that doesn't dull.
Iconography: Bodiless Intoxication. Eyes heavy-lidded, gently swaying. The subtle drunkenness of delight, pleasure that loosens the grip of the literal mind.

**48 · Anaṅga-Madanāturā · अनङ्गमदनातुरा**
Quality: Pure Longing · Tattva: Virahāgni (separation-fire) · Location: Throat / sternum
Function: The longing-arrow — Kāma's most subtle weapon. The wound that opens rather than closes.
Somatic: The throat tightening with sweetness. The eyes filling without sadness. The chest opening toward what cannot be reached.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Madanāturā (afflicted-by-Kāma / longing) — 'the bodiless longing,' ache without object.
Phrase: Thank you for the longing that is its own answer.
Iconography: Bodiless Love-Longing. A hand at her heart, leaning toward the absent. Pure longing — the ache of wanting that is itself sweet.

**49 · Anaṅga-Rekhā · अनङ्गरेखा**
Quality: Suggested Form · Tattva: Sūkṣmākṛti (subtle outline) · Location: Eyes / spine inclining
Function: The trace that organizes recognition. The suggestion that calls forth attention.
Somatic: The eye following an unspoken curve. The body inclining toward an implied shape.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Rekhā (line / streak / curve) — 'the bodiless line,' the trace that suggests form.
Phrase: Thank you for the line that suggests without saying.
Iconography: Bodiless Line. A single line drawn in the air. The first suggestion of form — the outline before the figure, beauty implied rather than stated.

**50 · Anaṅga-Veginī · अनङ्गवेगिनी**
Quality: Subtle Velocity · Tattva: Vega (subtle impulse) · Location: Heart / first reflexes
Function: The impulse-arrow. The vector quality of subtle attraction.
Somatic: The way the heart leaps before the mind has registered the beloved face. The sudden lean-toward.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Vegin (swift / impulsive) + ī — 'the bodiless swift one,' velocity without substance.
Phrase: Thank you for the velocity that crosses distance without measuring it.
Iconography: Bodiless Swiftness. Caught mid-motion, garments streaming. The velocity of attraction — the quickness with which desire crosses distance.

**51 · Anaṅga-Aṅkuśā · अनङ्गाङ्कुशा**
Quality: Subtle Hook · Tattva: Smaraṇa (subtle remembering) · Location: Heart hook / between the eyes
Function: The directing arrow. Keeps attention on the field of recognition.
Somatic: The slight tug at the heart when distracted from what matters. The course-correction that arrives without judgment.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Aṅkuśa (goad / hook) + ā — 'the bodiless goad,' the hook without barb.
Phrase: Thank you for the hook that returns me.
Iconography: Bodiless Goad. A small jeweled hook in hand. The gentle pull that steers — desire as the goad that directs the wandering toward its object.

**52 · Anaṅga-Mālinī · अनङ्गमालिनी**
Quality: Subtle Adornment · Tattva: Saundarya (innate beauty) · Location: Whole field / perceptual atmosphere
Function: The closing arrow of the 3rd avarana. Garlands what came before — recognition that beauty is the field itself.
Somatic: The way an ordinary object becomes beautiful when seen rightly. The flower in the cracked vase, fully glorious.
Etymology: Anaṅga + Mālinī (garlanded one) — 'the bodiless garlanded,' wreathed without wreath.
Phrase: Thank you for the adornment that needs no adorning.
Iconography: Bodiless Garland. Weaving a garland of flowers. The adornment that completes — subtle beauty strung into a whole. She closes the eight-petal ring.

# RING 4 — Sarvasaubhāgyadāyaka (14) — 14 triangles · Diamond on fat · Lunar fortnight · Heart · water

*Sampradāya — the lineage. Cosmic-scale forms. Several are Devī-forms of Ring 1 Mudrās: where the Mudrā moved one mind, the Devī moves all of creation.*

**53 · Sarva-Saṅkṣobhiṇī (Devī) · सर्वसंक्षोभिणी**
Quality: Cosmic Stirring · Tattva: Kṣobha (cosmic agitation) · Location: Whole field beginning to move
Function: Stirs the field of Sthiti into its first movement of sustaining.
Somatic: The full-body resonance that begins when the gesture takes hold. From a tremor to a wave.
Etymology: Sarva (all) + Saṅkṣobhiṇī (the agitating one) — 'she who agitates all.' As Devi: the cosmic force the 1st-avarana Mudra invokes.
Phrase: Thank you for the stirring that has become a goddess.
Iconography: The cosmic stirring — crimson and vast, hands churning the deep. Where her Mudrā-sister stirred one mind, she agitates all of creation into motion. The universe-wide impulse that ends stillness so the play can begin.

**54 · Sarva-Vidrāviṇī (Devī) · सर्वविद्राविणी**
Quality: Cosmic Flow · Tattva: Dravatā (fluidity itself) · Location: Joints, lymph, all fluids
Function: Liquefies fixed patterns at the lineage-transmission level.
Somatic: The body remembering it is 70% water. The breath remembering it is air finding water.
Etymology: Sarva + Vidrāviṇī (the liquefier) — 'she who liquefies all.' Devi-form of the 2nd-opening Mudra.
Phrase: Thank you for the flow that makes life possible.
Iconography: The cosmic melting — flowing and molten, dissolving fixed forms across the whole field. She liquefies whatever has hardened anywhere, returning the rigid to flow at the scale of worlds.

**55 · Sarva-Ākarṣiṇī (Devī) · सर्वाकर्षिणी**
Quality: Universal Magnetism · Tattva: Ākarṣaṇa (universal attraction) · Location: Heart / center of gravity
Function: The mother-form of the 16 Karshinis. All attraction stems from Her.
Somatic: The way everything wants to come home. The gravitational pull of recognition.
Etymology: Sarva + Ākarṣiṇī (the all-attractor) — 'she who attracts all.' The cosmic source of the 16 Karshinis.
Phrase: Thank you for the attraction that births the sixteen daughters.
Iconography: The universal magnet — radiant, both hands beckoning, all things in creation leaning toward her. Attraction itself at cosmic scale, the gravity by which the scattered returns to center.

**56 · Sarva-Āhlādinī · सर्वाह्लादिनी**
Quality: Cosmic Joy · Tattva: Ānanda (substrate-bliss) · Location: Face / chest
Function: Sustains existence through delight. Without Her, sthiti would be mere duration.
Somatic: The unbidden smile. The lightness that arrives independent of circumstance.
Etymology: Sarva + Āhlāda (joy / delight) + inī — 'she who delights all.' No 1st-avarana counterpart — new to the 4th ring.
Phrase: Thank you for the joy that has no reason.
Iconography: Cosmic joy — glowing rose, half-laughing, gladness pouring outward. She floods all beings with delight, the universe rejoicing in its own existence.

**57 · Sarva-Sammohinī · सर्वसम्मोहिनी**
Quality: Divine Bewilderment · Tattva: Moha (sacred bewilderment) · Location: Forehead / behind the eyes
Function: Periodically disrupts the patterns of Sthiti so they don't crystallize into rigidity.
Somatic: The moment thought loses its grip. The pause when categories don't quite fit. The space before knowing returns.
Etymology: Sarva + Sammohanī (bewilderer / enchanter of senses) — 'she who bewilders all' — the divine sweet confusion that breaks habitual thought.
Phrase: Thank you for the bewilderment that breaks the trance.
Iconography: Divine bewilderment — lovely and veiling, a soft mist about her. She enchants and confounds, the sacred māyā that makes the One forget itself in order to play at being many.

**58 · Sarva-Sthambhinī · सर्वस्तम्भिनी**
Quality: Cosmic Stillness · Tattva: Stambha (cosmic pillar) · Location: Diaphragm / breath turn
Function: Holds the field stable so other forces can act.
Somatic: The breath suspended at the turn. The mind quieted between thoughts. The body utterly still in motion.
Etymology: Sarva + Sthambhinī (paralyzer / stabilizer) — 'she who stabilizes all.' The pause that is also strength.
Phrase: Thank you for the stillness that holds movement.
Iconography: Cosmic stillness — utterly motionless, palm raised in arrest. She holds all things suspended, the power to stop time and motion across the entire field.

**59 · Sarva-Jṛmbhinī · सर्वजृम्भिणी**
Quality: Cosmic Expansion · Tattva: Vikāsa (expansion) · Location: Whole body extending
Function: Counterpart to Sthambhinī — Her expansion balances Sthambhinī's stillness.
Somatic: The yawn that opens the body. The unconscious stretch toward more space.
Etymology: Sarva + Jṛmbha (yawn / stretch / expansion) + inī — 'she who expands all,' the yawning-open. Counterpart to Sthambhinī.
Phrase: Thank you for the expansion that comes from the depths.
Iconography: Cosmic expansion — stretching wide as if waking, space unfolding from her limbs. She is the great yawn of creation, the opening-out by which the compressed becomes vast.

**60 · Sarva-Vaśaṅkarī (Devī) · सर्ववशंकरी**
Quality: Cosmic Yielding · Tattva: Vaśya (cosmic resonance) · Location: Heart-field / radiance from chest
Function: Sustains the world through resonance rather than coercion.
Somatic: The way everything around you settles when you are aligned. The natural ordering.
Etymology: Sarva + Vaśaṅkarī (the subjugator-yielder) — 'she who brings all under influence.' Devi-form of the 4th-opening Mudra.
Phrase: Thank you for the influence that needs no force.
Iconography: Cosmic yielding — serene, an open palm, all things consenting before her. The universe-scale power by which everything comes willingly under sway, without force.

**61 · Sarva-Rañjinī · सर्वरञ्जिनी**
Quality: Cosmic Coloring · Tattva: Rañjana (aesthetic charge) · Location: Eyes / ears / aesthetic field
Function: The aesthetic dimension of sthiti. Maintains the rasa-charge of existence.
Somatic: The world becoming saturated. Colors deepening. Sounds gaining timbre.
Etymology: Sarva + Rañj (to color / delight) + inī — 'she who colors all,' the chromatic-aesthetic principle.
Phrase: Thank you for the coloring that makes life vivid.
Iconography: Cosmic coloring — a brush of pigment in hand, tinting the colorless. She dyes the world with feeling and hue, the power that makes bare existence beautiful and felt.

**62 · Sarva-Onmādinī (Devī) · सर्वोन्मादिनी**
Quality: Cosmic Intoxication · Tattva: Unmāda (divine intoxication) · Location: Third eye / aesthetic field
Function: Sustains the heightened state of devotional recognition.
Somatic: Heightened seeing. Tasting the world. Reality becoming dreamlike but more vivid.
Etymology: Sarva + Onmādinī (the intoxicator) — 'she who intoxicates all.' Devi-form of the 5th-opening Mudra.
Phrase: Thank you for the intoxication that doesn't intoxicate.
Iconography: Cosmic intoxication — swaying with divine wine, eyes alight. She maddens all creation with bliss, the rapture that loosens the universe from its literalism.

**63 · Sarvārthasādhikā · सर्वार्थसाधिका**
Quality: Cosmic Fulfillment · Tattva: Sādhana-siddhi (accomplishment-fruit) · Location: Solar plexus / center of action
Function: The realizing principle. Brings the practitioner's deepest aims to fruition.
Somatic: The way things resolve when you stop trying. The natural completion.
Etymology: Sarva + Artha (purpose / aim) + Sādhikā (accomplisher) — 'she who accomplishes all purposes.'
Phrase: Thank you for the accomplishment that comes without striving.
Iconography: Cosmic fulfillment — a wish-granting gem in hand, gaze steady. She accomplishes all aims, the power by which every true purpose is carried to completion.

**64 · Sarva-Sampatpūraṇī · सर्वसम्पत्पूरणी**
Quality: Cosmic Abundance · Tattva: Pūrṇatā (fullness) · Location: Belly / abundance-field
Function: Maintains the felt-sense of abundance that allows generosity.
Somatic: The full belly. The full chest. The sense of having enough at the cellular level.
Etymology: Sarva + Sampat (wealth / abundance) + Pūraṇī (filler) — 'she who fills all with abundance.'
Phrase: Thank you for the abundance that is the field itself.
Iconography: Cosmic abundance — hands overflowing with grain and gold. She fills all lack, the power that pours fullness into every empty place.

**65 · Sarva-Mantramayī · सर्वमन्त्रमयी**
Quality: Cosmic Mantra · Tattva: Mantra-svarūpa (mantric form of reality) · Location: Throat / breath-rhythm
Function: The sounded-aspect of sthiti. All speech is Her.
Somatic: The way ordinary speech reveals itself as music. The rhythm of breath as recitation.
Etymology: Sarva + Mantra + Mayī (made-of) — 'she made of all mantras,' the embodied principle of sacred speech.
Phrase: Thank you for the mantric quality of being itself.
Iconography: Cosmic mantra — made entirely of sound, syllables shimmering across her form. She is reality woven of vibration, the power that holds creation together as living word.

**66 · Sarva-Dvandvakṣayaṅkarī · सर्वद्वन्द्वक्षयंकरी**
Quality: Cosmic Non-Duality · Tattva: Advaita (non-duality) · Location: Heart / non-dual point
Function: Closes the 4th ring by dissolving the opposites Sthiti has been holding in tension.
Somatic: The moment opposites stop feeling opposed. The recognition that one is the other.
Etymology: Sarva + Dvandva (duality / pair-of-opposites) + Kṣaya (destruction) + Kāriṇī (maker) — 'she who destroys all pairs of opposites.'
Phrase: Thank you for the dissolving of duality.
Iconography: Cosmic non-duality — calm and undivided, hands joining opposites into one. She dissolves all pairs — pleasure and pain, self and other — the power that ends division. She closes the fourteen-triangle.

# RING 6 — Sarvarakṣākara (10) — Inner 10 · Ruby on marrow · Season · Forehead · ether

**84 · Sarvānandamayī · सर्वानन्दमयी**

# RING 7 — Sarvarogahara (12) — 8 triangles (Vāgdevī) + 4 Weapons · Pearl on śukra · Solar half-year · Crown · air

*Eight Vāsinīs — the speech-goddesses, governing the syllables at the level of inner speech. Then four Weapons — Lalitā's own instruments.*

# RING 9 — Sarvānandamaya (1) — Bindu · All gems, pure light · Tejas · Kāla–Akāla · Totality · light

**102 · Mahātripurasundarī · महात्रिपुरसुन्दरी**
Quality: The Source-Beauty · Tattva: Para-Bindu (the supreme point) · Bīja: Hrīm — or the full Pañcadaśī: Ka E Ī La Hrīm | Ha Sa Ka Ha La Hrīm | Sa Ka La Hrīm · Location: The point at which all bodily locations converge. The heart-bindu.
Function: The Source. The Bindu. The point that contains all 101 previous Shaktis as Her movements. Beyond function — She is the field in which all functioning happens.

She is Lalitā, the Source-Beauty — the One whom every Śakti of every ring was, all along, drawing toward. Beyond attainment, because she is the attainer. Here the journey inward arrives: there was never anyone here but Her.
